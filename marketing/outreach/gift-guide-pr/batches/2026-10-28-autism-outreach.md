## What approving this item does

- `approved` approves sending every pitch listed below, unchanged. Executor: outreach-gift-guide-pr. It sends nothing itself. For each pitch it either writes the exact plain text and the contact form or profile URL in its report, for Eden to paste (every route found is a form, a contact page or a public profile), or, if Eden finds a public email on the contact page, creates a Gmail draft (HTML as htmlBody, plain text as body) for Eden to send. Then it adds a contacts-log row.
- `changes: <slug> <note>` (one or more slugs) sends back only the named pitches; every other pitch stays approved. A `changes:` note naming no slug sends the whole batch back. `rejected` rejects all.
- **Kits are not approved here.** Rows 1 and 2 offer a free 16×16 kit. They are sent only if their own `offer` item is approved too (see the Kit column). If the kit is rejected, that pitch is rewritten without a kit and proposed again.
- Each row's Hash is `git hash-object` of the .md and .html at proposal time. If a pitch file changes, that pitch needs approval again.

## Before you approve

- Open each contact route and confirm it is live. Pages could not be opened from this environment, so every detail is from search text (`unverified`).
- No pitch claims Memobrick treats, helps or is therapy for anything. Each one says the bricks are small. No quotes, reviews or customer numbers are used.
