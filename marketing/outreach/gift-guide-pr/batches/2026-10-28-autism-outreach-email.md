- `approved` approves sending every pitch listed below, unchanged. Executor: outreach-gift-guide-pr. It sends nothing itself: for each pitch it creates a Gmail draft to the listed email (the .html as htmlBody, the .md text as body) for Eden to send from Gmail, then adds a contacts-log row.

- No kit is offered in any of these pitches. All are commission only (Shopify Collabs, 10% commission and a 10% audience code) or editorial.
