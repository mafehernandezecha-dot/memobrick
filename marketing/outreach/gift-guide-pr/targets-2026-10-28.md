Asked by Eden through the coordinator on 2026-10-28: reach a new audience (autistic kids and adults, their families, occupational therapists and special-education teachers). Rules come from `marketing/seo/briefs/2026-10-28-autism-calm-building-research.md`: never claim Memobrick treats, helps or is therapy for autism or anything medical; autistic people speak for themselves; no invented quotes; small bricks warning; lead with useful help, the product second.

## How this list was checked, and its limits

- **Web search only.** Page fetches fail in this session (DNS blocked for every outlet domain tried today: lifeskillsadvocate.com, neurodivergentinsights.com, theottoolbox.com). So no page was opened. Every detail below comes from search-result text and is `unverified` until Eden opens the page. "Active" means search shows a dated post, book or guide in 2025 or 2026. Where only older activity was found, the row says so.
- **Contact routes are public contact pages, forms or public profiles only.** No private email or phone number was copied. Where the exact contact page was not seen in search, the row says "not seen" and names where it should be, for Eden to confirm before sending.
- **contacts-log.md is empty**, so nobody is blocked by the 30-day rule. None of these targets appear in earlier targets files.
- **Audience-fit check, adapted for this audience.** `buyer-profile.md` describes today's buyer (US women 35 to 64 buying a $35 to $150 photo gift). This run targets a new audience on purpose, so each target is scored 1 to 5 on: readers (autistic people, their families, OTs or teachers who choose activities), country (US first), price fit ($69.88 16×16 kit and up), content fit (gift guides, activities, honest product views), and whether readers act (gift guides, affiliate links, shop pages, a creator program). Score 4 or 5 gets a pitch, 3 is "maybe, needs Eden", 1 and 2 are dropped. Organizations that do not do product promotions are listed and not pitched.
- **Kit or commission (owner rule, 2026-10-14).** The default is commission only (Shopify Collabs, 10% commission and a 10% audience code). Because a creator or OT can only give an honest view after building one, the no-kit pitches ask instead for a free look at our web editor's mosaic preview, which costs them nothing. A kit is proposed only where search shows real reach and a clear fit, each as its own `offer` item. Two are proposed (rows 1 and 2). Product facts: 16×16 build-at-home kit, 2,500 pieces, about 2.5 hours, $69.88 (Shopify, checked live 2026-10-28).
- **Language.** Pitches use identity-first language ("autistic people", "autistic builders"), the common preference of autistic self-advocates. No age is stated, because product-facts.md has no age warning. Pitches say the bricks are small and the kit is not for young children who still put things in their mouths.
