## How it was checked, and its limits

- Outlet pages still cannot be opened from this environment (DNS fails on every outlet domain; WebFetch on greenweddingshoes.com failed again today), and Similarweb and Ahrefs failed to connect. - Scale: 5 = clearly our buyer on readers, country, price, content and buying. 4 = readers, country and price fit, one point unproven. 3 = mixed: **maybe, needs Eden**. 1 or 2 = drop.

## What this means for sales

Our data shows no gift guide or blog has ever sent meaningful orders (0 editorial sites in the top 25 order referrers over 12 months). The three proposed pitches cost nothing up front and pay only on sales. The best upside is the two national desks: they feature our exact category at our price to US gift buyers. An editor address for Shop TODAY or CNN Underscored is worth more than any number of small blogs.
