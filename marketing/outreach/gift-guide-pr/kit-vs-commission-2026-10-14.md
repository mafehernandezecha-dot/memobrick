# Kit vs commission review, 2026-10-14

Requested by Eden on 2026-10-14: "re-analyze who to give a kit and who just to offer earning on commission. Not everyone should get a free kit."

## Rule used

- **KIT** only with evidence of real reach and clear fit (a published gift guide with real traffic, an editor who reviews physical products, or strong engagement with families, couples, or gift buyers), where one placement can plausibly bring sales well above the kit's cost. Every kit gets its own `offer` approval item, separate from the pitch.
- **COMMISSION ONLY** is the default: an invite to the Memobrick creator program on Shopify Collabs, 10% commission on every sale plus the creator's own code giving their audience 10% off (the only tier; never offer more). Public application page: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/collabs (checked live today; its "Apply now" button opens the Shopify Collabs community application for Memobrick).
- **SKIP** when the target is not worth pitching now (paid placement, duplicate of a stronger pitch to the same outlet, no workable route, or weak fit).

## How the evidence was gathered, and its limits

Outlet pages still cannot be opened from this environment (every outlet domain fails), and the traffic tools (Similarweb, Ahrefs) failed to connect. All evidence below comes from web search text and the outlets' own self-reported figures, so it is `unverified`. Where no audience figure was found, the row says so. That lack of evidence is itself a reason for commission only.
