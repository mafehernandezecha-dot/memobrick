# Memobrick buyer profile

Built 2026-10-14 by outreach-gift-guide-pr (Mario) at Eden's request, from data, not assumptions. Every outlet or creator must be checked against this profile before a pitch is written (see `audience-fit-2026-10-14.md`). Refresh it each quarter or when the data snapshots in `marketing/data/` start arriving.

## Sources and limits

- **Shopify analytics (ShopifyQL, read-only, aggregated only).** Last 12 months = 2025-10-15 to 2026-10-13. Last holiday = 2025-11-01 to 2025-12-31. No names, emails or addresses were read or copied.
- **Meta ads (PhotoBrick account, read-only).** Purchases by age and gender, Nov 1 to Dec 31 2025. These are Meta-attributed purchases only (417), and they follow who we targeted: about 82% of spend went to women. Cost per purchase was similar for men and women, so the female share partly reflects targeting.
- **Ads analyst audit** (`marketing/analytics/ads-analyst/google-audit-2026-10-13.md`) and **growth analyst diagnosis** (`marketing/analytics/growth-analyst/2026-10-11-yoy-diagnosis.md`) for search terms, landing pages and last season's weekly shape.
- **Not available:** `marketing/data/` has no GA4 or Meta audience snapshots yet (the morning pull has never run). Orders have no gift note or occasion field: the only line item attribute is the uploaded photo file. "For whom" below is inferred from discount codes, ad themes and product mix, and is marked as inferred.

## The 3 lines that best describe our buyer

1. **A US woman aged 35 to 64 (mostly 35 to 54), buying a personalized photo gift for her family: her partner, her kids, her parents, or the family pet.**
2. **She spends $70 to $150 (average order $97), most often on the 16×16 ($69.88) or 20×20 ($99.88) kit, and she buys for an occasion: Christmas above all, then Valentine's Day and Mother's Day.**
3. **She finds us through Google search, Facebook and Instagram and buys on her phone; no gift guide or blog has ever sent us meaningful orders, so every PR placement is a test that has to reach her exactly.**

## For whom and why (inferred)

- **Family.** The best Meta ad of the last 28 days is the family photo ad (8 purchases); last year's Christmas weeks carried 44% of the year's orders. Christmas family gifts are the core.
- **Couples.** BEMYVALENTINE is the most used code of the year (122 orders), ahead of every holiday code; an "anniversary" ad and a "marriage" video are among the active ads. Partners buy for each other at Valentine's Day and anniversaries.
- **Moms and grandparents.** MOMINBRICKS (Mother's Day) 32 orders; May had 155 orders. Buyers aged 45 to 64 are likely buying for parents, grandchildren or their own home.
- **Pets.** A dog video and a "dog" ad run in the account (the dog video has one purchase in 28 days). Pets are a real but smaller, unproven theme.
- **Why:** a sentimental, one-of-a-kind gift made from their own photo, plus an activity to build together. The product is bought as a gift and a keepsake, not as a toy.
- **Not our buyer:** people looking for LEGO toys, free or printable templates, or LEGO album covers. Those searches spent about $172 in 28 days with 0 purchases (ads audit). Brick-hobby and toy-collector audiences are not our audience.

- Average order value $96.58 (12 months). Comfort zone $70 to $150. Above $200 is rare.
- 67% of orders used no discount code (1,686 of 2,523). Codes help (FLASHSALE 98, HELLOFALL 57, HOLIDAYPREP 48, cyber20 45) but the gift drives the sale, not the discount. A frugal or deal-hunting audience is a weaker match.

- **Honest point for PR:** we have no record of a gift guide or blog bringing orders. Placements are worth trying, but only where the readers are this buyer, and a free kit has to be justified by real reach among her.

## Audience-fit test for any outlet or creator

Score 1 to 5 on these questions (see `audience-fit-2026-10-14.md` for the scored list):

1. **Readers:** mostly women 35 to 64, parents, grandparents, partners, or pet owners who buy gifts? (Wedding planners aged 25 to 34, teens, toy collectors and LEGO hobbyists are weaker.)
2. **Country:** mostly US readers?
3. **Price:** do they feature personalized or photo gifts at $35 to $150?
4. **Content:** gift guides, product reviews, or family activity content, published before Nov 10 or for Valentine's or Mother's Day?
5. **Buying:** do readers click through and buy (affiliate links, shop pages, "where to buy" lists), rather than read for inspiration only?

5 = clearly yes on all five. 4 = yes on readers, country and price, and one other point unproven. 3 = mixed (maybe, needs Eden). 1 or 2 = not our buyer (drop).
