## How this list was checked, and its limits

- **Web search only.** Outlet pages cannot be opened from this session (WebFetch fails with DNS errors). Every detail is from search-result text and is `unverified` until Eden glances at the page.
- **Contact confirmed** means the search result showed the exact contact page URL or a public business email on the outlet's own site. Each email row names the page it was seen on. "Partly" means the contact page URL was seen but no email, or the email is a general support inbox. No personal or private emails or phone numbers were recorded. Where an email was only in a third-party directory (FeedSpot masks them), it was not counted.
- **Scoring** is the same as the morning list (readers, US first, price fit for a $69.88 16×16 kit and up, content fit, whether readers act). Score 4 or 5 gets a pitch, 3 is "maybe, needs Eden", 1 and 2 are dropped.
- **Kits:** none proposed in this batch. Reach figures found are old or missing (see Kit decisions). All pitches are commission only (Shopify Collabs, 10% and 10%) or editorial, and the no-kit ask is a free look at the editor preview.
- **Product facts:** 16×16 kit, 2,500 pieces, about 2.5 hours, $69.88 (Shopify, checked live 2026-10-28 in the morning run). Order by December 15 (Facts table).
