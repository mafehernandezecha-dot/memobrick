**Subject:** A teacher's honest view on a color-map brick build?

Hi Christine,

Many special-education teachers learn to set up structured, visual activities from your blog and podcast, so I would value your view.

Memobrick turns a photo into a DIY brick mosaic kit. The builder follows a printed color map and presses small 1×1 bricks onto a baseplate, row by row, until the picture appears.

What the build is like:

- One brick size, labeled color bags, the same step every time, and a clear finish.
- Roles can be split: one reads the map, one finds colors, one builds.
- A 16×16 kit is 2,500 pieces, about two and a half hours. The bricks are small, so it is not for young children who still put things in their mouths.

We make no claims about outcomes. Would this format work as a calm activity in the classrooms you support, and what would you change? The mosaic preview is free with any photo at mymemobrick.com.
