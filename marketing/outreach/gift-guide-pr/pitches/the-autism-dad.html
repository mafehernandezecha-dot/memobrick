I'm Eden from Memobrick. Your months-long Mightier review made me think you are the right dad to tell us the truth about our kits.

Memobrick turns a photo into a DIY brick mosaic. You follow a printed color map and press small 1×1 bricks onto a baseplate until the picture appears: a pet, a person, a favorite place.

■One brick size, labeled color bags, the same step repeated, and a clear finish.
■It can be built alone or as a team: one reads the map, one sorts colors, one builds.
■The bricks are small, so it is not for young children who still put things in their mouths.

We make no therapy claims. Does this format suit your family, or not? "This isn't for us" is a useful answer too. The mosaic preview is free with any photo at mymemobrick.com.
