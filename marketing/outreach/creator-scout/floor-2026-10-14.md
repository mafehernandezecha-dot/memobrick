# Engagement floor proposal, 2026-10-14

Approving this item sets María Beatriz's engagement floor to **2% real engagement**, replacing the temporary 10% line. Real engagement means the median of (likes + comments + saves) / followers over the last ~12 posts, after removing giveaways, spikes and bot-comment posts. A creator still has to pass every other check (real audience, fit, content, location, no red flags). The floor only decides who moves from Maybe to Approve.

## What I measured

- 452 pending applications. 45 read in detail. Engagement below is Collabs' figure for each applicant's main account, because I measured post-level real engagement for one creator only.
- At or below 10%: **43 of 45**. The 2 above 10% are not real: Olivia Bennett (13.04% on an Instagram that no longer exists) and Nguyen Thi Truc My (13.64% on an account with 6 followers).
- Distribution of the 45 (main account):
  - 0% to 0.1%: 24 (the coupon and fake-account group; accounts with no audience or no account counted here)
  - 0.1% to 1%: 10
  - 1% to 2%: 3
  - 2% to 5%: 3 (Niki Loheit 3.23%, Baldo Vivian 3.09%, Alina Macare 4.36%)
  - above 5%: 5, all tiny or gone accounts (Sophia Hayes and Ebony Grice 5.09% on 33 followers, Bao Long Luong 10% on 2 followers, Olivia Bennett 13.04% on a deleted account, Nguyen Thi Truc My 13.64% on 6 followers)
- Niki Loheit, the one creator measured post by post: **2.7%** median over 4 posts (likes + comments / 1,854 followers).
- Proof that this range sells: Memobrick's best Collabs creator so far, Evan Rubin (legocr8tor, 111K, Collabs engagement 3.3%), brought **24 orders and $1,787.10** before his connection was paused on 2023-10-23.

## Recommendation: 2%

- Healthy real engagement on Instagram is usually 1% to 5%, and it falls as accounts grow. Almost no real creator is above 10%, so the 10% line keeps every good creator in Maybe forever.
- The fakes in this queue are at 0% to 1%, far below 2%. The few that score higher are caught by the other checks (no audience, duplicate accounts, coupon bios).
- 2% sits under the two real LEGO creators we know (Evan 3.3%, Niki 2.7%) and above the fake-account cluster.
