## Summary
Research run for Eden (chat, 2026-10-21): which Facebook groups hold Memobrick's buyers. About 35 groups found and scored, Top 15 picked, a 5-group 2-week test proposed below.
Facebook itself could not be opened from the sandbox, so member counts come from search results and directories, and nearly every group's rules are not verified. The test begins with a read-only check of the rules.
Nothing was joined, posted, commented on, or messaged.

## Done
- Research: `marketing/social/planner/research/2026-10-21-facebook-groups.md`. It covers every group found, by category (dogs and cats, pet loss, moms and family, crafts, homeschool, LEGO fans, gift and Christmas), with URL, privacy, members and their source, promotion rules, the kind of post each group would welcome, and scores for fit, size and openness. Also the Top 15, the recommended 5, the plan for taking part honestly (who posts, value-first ratio, rotation, answering "where did you get it", tracked links, Shopify measurement, risks), and sources.
- Test proposal with 3 example posts: `marketing/social/planner/research/2026-10-21-facebook-groups-test.md`.
- Main findings:
  - Pet groups (dog moms, breed groups, large cat groups) and photo-keeping craft groups (scrapbooking, DIY) match our buyer best.
  - I found no real gift-idea, Christmas-gift, couples, or grandparent groups. Search returned only Facebook Pages, which can't be posted in.
  - Pet loss groups fit the memorial buyer, but they are grief spaces. They are left out on purpose.
  - LEGO adult-fan groups score low. The buyer profile shows brick-hobby searches had 0 purchases, and those groups ban selling.
  - Handmade "promo day" groups are mostly sellers talking to sellers. Not recommended.
- The copy check passes on both files.

## Sales impact
Facebook is already our best social channel for orders (20 orders in 90 days, about 3 times Instagram). Referred traffic is down 60% year on year, which is the top weak point. Groups are a free way to reach US women 35 to 64 with pets and family photos before the Nov 10 demand jump. The expected effect is small and unproven: a few tracked orders a week at best from 5 groups. It costs Eden about 20 minutes a day. The Nov 11 readout decides whether to keep going. If the test is not approved, nothing is lost: the research stays for later.
