Why a photo makes a meaningful Christmas gift

Most of us have hundreds of photos on our phones that nobody sees. The ones that matter most are usually simple: a laugh at the dinner table, a first dance, a dog asleep on the couch.

A gift made from one of those photos says "I remember this too." That is hard to get from something picked off a shelf. We wrote more about this in why personalized gifts feel so much better than generic gifts.

How a custom brick mosaic works

A Memobrick is a DIY brick mosaic kit made from your own photo. Here is the whole process.

Upload a photo and see your mosaic

Upload your photo on our website. The website creates your mosaic design and shows you a preview, so you can see how your photo looks in bricks before you order. If you get stuck, personal help is available.

Choose your options and order the kit

Pick the options that suit your photo and your space, then order your kit. Frames are sold separately.

Build it and display it

Your kit arrives with everything needed to build your design. Follow the building map, place the bricks, and you end up with a piece of art made from a moment you love.

How to choose the right photo

You do not need a professional picture. A few simple things help:

  • Clear faces. Pick a photo where the faces you care about are easy to see.
  • Good light. Daylight photos usually turn out better than dark indoor shots.
  • A moment that means something. The best photo is the one with a story behind it. Sometimes that is the silly one. Here is why your funniest photos might make the best keepsakes.

FAQ

What is a good personalized Christmas gift from a photo?

Choose something that keeps the photo in view and gives it meaning. A custom brick mosaic turns a favorite photo into wall art they build themselves, so it is both a keepsake and a holiday activity.

How does a custom brick mosaic work?

You upload a photo on our website, see your mosaic design, choose your options, and order a kit. Then you build the mosaic from 1×1 bricks using the building map.

What is included in a Memobrick kit?

Kits include the baseplates, 1×1 bricks, building map or instructions, and tool needed for your design.

Can I see my mosaic before I order?

Yes. The website shows you a preview of your mosaic design after you upload your photo, so you can try different photos before you order.

Is a frame included?

Frames are optional and sold separately. You can check the options when you choose your kit.

When should I order for Christmas?
