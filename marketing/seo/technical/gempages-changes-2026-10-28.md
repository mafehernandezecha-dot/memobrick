Why: in the HTML Google downloads, 35 GemPages articles have 3 links or fewer and 13 have none at all. The product cards and buttons are GemPages widgets, not normal links, so the articles send no visitors' path or ranking strength to the editor, the free-proof page or the products. A plain text link fixes that.
