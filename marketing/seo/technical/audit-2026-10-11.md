# Technical SEO audit: mymemobrick.com, 2026-10-11 (DRY RUN)

## How this audit was done

- **The live HTML could not be fetched.** `curl` was refused by the egress proxy (403, policy denial for mymemobrick.com). WebFetch failed DNS lookup for every host, example.com included. Nothing was rendered: no robots.txt, no sitemap.xml, no response headers, no Core Web Vitals.
- **Live store state was read through the Shopify Admin API (read only).** Store: Memobrick, domain mymemobrick.com, main theme `memobrick-theme-v25` (updated 2026-10-04). The audit covers:
  - every article (59, of which 54 are published), blog (5), page (73) and collection (7)
  - a sample of 31 products
  - the main custom product `1-big-board`
  - URL redirects under `/blogs`
  - the live theme files `layout/theme.liquid` and `templates/product.liquid`
- **Repo templates match the live theme.** The MD5 checksums of 55 live theme files were compared with the repo theme folder `memobrick-size-zoom-test-latest (2)/`:
  - All 49 `templates/article.*` files are byte-identical to live, so the article analysis below reflects what the live site serves.
  - Two files differ: `layout/theme.liquid` and `templates/product.liquid`. For those the live versions were read.
- **Checklist:** `searchfit-seo:technical-seo`.
- **Limits:** heading counts for GemPages articles come from static template parsing and are a heuristic. Rendered DOM was not checked.

## Issues ranked by likely sales impact

### 1. Critical: Trust claims and structured data are hardcoded or unverified

**Product schema.** Live `templates/product.liquid` emits a hardcoded rating on every product page:

```
"aggregateRating": { "@type": "AggregateRating", "ratingValue": "4.8", "reviewCount": "747" }
```

- It applies to all 280+ products, including bags of bricks and art kits with no reviews of their own.
- This breaks Google's review-snippet policy, because the rating is not for that specific item. A manual action would remove star ratings from every product result, right before the holiday peak.
- If Loox also outputs rating schema, there may be duplicate conflicting ratings. This could not be verified without rendered HTML.

**Visible claims that conflict with the brand file.**

- **"Made & shipped from Miami".** It appears 4 times on every product page: in the trust row, a badge, a note and the FAQ. The `offers-calendar.md` Facts table marks the Miami claim as unknown and unverified.
- **"Free proof before production" and "Our designers build it for you".** These appear on the product page, in the homepage meta fallback ("free design proof before production") and in the free-preview meta ("from a real designer"). The brand file says never imply a manual proof is required and never say a designer created the design. The owner needs to settle this conflict between the site and the brand rules.
- **Review and customer counts disagree across the site:**
  - theme: "747 reviews averaging 4.8"
  - article templates:
    - "+700 5-Star Reviews"
    - "1,015 Trusted Customer Reviews"
    - "1000+ Memobricks"
    - "22,500+ Happy Customers"
    - "30 Day Guarantee"
  - `baby-carrier-jul-10-22-19-45` (Baby's First Year) promises "Additional 10% off when buying from 2 pcs". That offer is not in the offers calendar.
  - `test-blog-post-07072026` carries "+700 5-Star Reviews / 22,500+ Happy Customers / 30 Day Guarantee" and tells readers to request a "Free Proof".
- **Meta description fallbacks in `layout/theme.liquid` contain em dashes.** This affects the homepage, blog, how-it-works, faqs and editor pages.

### 2. High: 47 GemPages blog articles have no Article schema, no breadcrumbs, and most have no meta description

**No Article schema or breadcrumbs.** 47 of 54 published articles render through GemPages templates (`article.gp-template-*` and `article.gem-*`). They replace `templates/article.liquid`, so they lose all of these:

- BlogPosting JSON-LD
- BreadcrumbList JSON-LD
- the byline and date
- the "Start designing your MemoBrick" CTA

No GemPages article template contains any `application/ld+json`.

**No meta descriptions.**

- 43 of the 44 `gp-template` articles have no SEO description set. Thanksgiving is the only one that has one.
- For GemPages articles the text lives in the template, not in the Shopify `body`. In the sample checked (the Christmas "favorite moment" article), `body` is empty. Wherever that holds, the theme's fallback (`article.content | truncate: 160`) outputs nothing, so the page ships **with no meta description at all**.
- Where a stale body exists, as in `test-blog-post-07072026`, the meta description describes text that is different from what the page shows.
- The blog index cards (`blog.liquid`, `excerpt_or_content`) also show no excerpt.

**Missing featured images.** 30 articles published 2026-09-06 have no featured image. They show as text-only cards on `/blogs/news`, and their share previews fall back to the theme default image.

**Author shown as "GemPages".** All GemPages articles list "GemPages" as the author. It does not render on GemPages templates, but it would show if they ever moved back to `article.liquid`.

### 3. High: Duplicate holiday and seasonal content is live (confirmed in live store data)

**Exact duplicates:**

- `/blogs/news/ikram-gempages-the-perfect-holiday-gift-for-mindful-creativity` was published 2026-09-10. Its SEO title is "[Ikram GemPages] The Perfect Holiday Gift for Mindful Creativity". Its body overlaps 98% with `/blogs/news/theperfect-holiday`. A third copy, `gem-backup-...`, is unpublished.
- `/pages/blog-post` is published and has 100% body overlap with `/blogs/news/duplicate-from-the-best-summer-vacation-souvenirs-arenand-39-t-bought-theyand-39-re-built`. Both carry a stale "Advertorial / Updated 26 October 2025" header.

**Christmas cannibalization.** 10 Christmas or holiday articles (including the hub) compete for similar queries. Three share the same "build it together" angle:

- `the-most-fun-christmas-gifts-are-the-ones-you-create-together`
- `give-more-than-a-gift-personalized-christmas-memories-to-build-together-yfpbkl`
- `this-christmas-give-them-a-memory-they-can-build`

Their text overlap is low (≤0.09 Jaccard), so they are not duplicates. They need a hub and interlinking, not a merge yet. See `article-inventory.md`.

**Weak internal linking.** GemPages articles link to almost nothing:

- 42 of 44 have no hard-coded links to other articles, collections or products. Their only links are GemPages Liquid buttons, whose targets could not be resolved statically.
- Only 2 articles link to other articles, both to the same three: `fathers-day...`, `looking-beyond-photo-frames...` and `test-blog-post-07072026`.

### 4. High: Redirect and broken-link problems

**Redirect count.** The Admin API's redirect count is capped at its maximum of 10,000, so the store has at least 10,000 redirect rules. Many are junk, for example `fbclid` variants of `/blogs/news/theperfect-holiday-thoughtful-gifts` sent to the homepage.

**Wrong redirect target.** `/blogs/news/why-personalized-gifts-create-stronger-emotional-connections` redirects to the graduation article. The article with that title actually lives at `/blogs/news/test-blog-post-07072026`.

**Broken link.** The graduation article body links to `/blogs/news/why-personalized-gifts-create-stronger-emotional-connections-2`. No article or redirect exists at that path, so it is likely a 404.

**Dormant redirect.** A redirect sends `/blogs/news/theperfect-holiday` to the homepage. It does nothing while the article is live, but would fire if the article were unpublished. Remove it before any consolidation.

**Missing template.** The graduation article is assigned to `article.gp-template-627595347056657074`, which does not exist in the live theme. Shopify falls back to `article.liquid`, which is actually fine. The unpublished Ikram copy points to a missing template too.

### 5. Medium: Heading structure on GemPages articles (heuristic)

- 19 of 44 GemPages articles have no `<h1>`.
- 3 have two H1 tags, the first one empty: `baby-carrier-jul-10-22-19-45`, `why-personalized-gifts-feel-so-much-better-than-generic-gifts` and `baby-carrier-aug-14-20-11-55`.
- 4 of the no-H1 articles have no H2 either: `looking-beyond-photo-frames...`, `the-best-keepsakes...`, `the-art-on-your-wall...` and `this-christmas-give-them-a-memory...`.

### 6. Medium: URL, title and blog structure hygiene

**Bad article handles:**

- `test-blog-post-07072026`
- `baby-carrier-jul-10-22-19-45`, which is about a baby's first year
- `baby-carrier-aug-14-20-11-55`, which is about riders
- `copy-of-why-your-funniest-photos-might-make-the-best-keepsakes`, which is titled "Made With Heart"
- four `duplicate-from-*` handles
- random suffixes such as `-kbstga`, `-ifqdgg`, `-vrrphy`, `-mxezkd`, `-qsfiky`, `-yfpbkl`, `-qcaqov` and `-aug-8-10`
- HTML entity residue such as `-arenand-39-t-` and `valentineand-39-s`

**Title problems:**

- The Valentine article title is stored with a literal `&#39;`.
- The Fox News article SEO title is `** Named One of the Best Holiday Gifts by Fox News** - `.
- Several auto-generated SEO titles end in a dangling ` - `.
- The "Memorial blog" title does not match its H1, "Healing After Losing a Pet".

**Blog structure:**

- Two blogs each hold one article, giving deep, duplicated URLs: `/blogs/experience-the-ultimate-bonding-activity-with-memobrick/...` and `/blogs/the-top-superlative-ways-to-disconnect-kids-from-technology/...`.
- Two blogs are empty: `memobrick-can-help-you-have-more-quality-moment` and `the-ideal-present`. Each renders a thin "No posts yet" page.

### 7. Medium: Duplicate catalog pages

**Duplicate products.** Products with `-1` twins and identical titles and SEO fields (sample of 31; likely more exist):

- `abstract-face-bricked-mosaic-home-decor-20x30-1`
- `abstract-woman-bricked-mosaic-home-decor-20x30-1`
- `baboon-abstract-bricked-mosaic-home-decor-20x30-1`

**Duplicate collection.** `avada-best-sellers` has the same 280 products as `newest-products`. Its SEO title is "AVADA - Best Sellers Get it now" and its meta description is blank (" - Memobrick").

**Product meta and text:**

- Many product meta descriptions are auto-truncated ("...Our artists have created a special - Memobrick"), contain no spaces between spec fields, or use "LEGO®-style", which carries trademark risk.
- Product image alt text is just the product title. On the main product page, gallery thumbnails get "title — photo N", which includes an em dash.

### 8. Medium: Mobile speed signals (not measured)

- `layout/theme.liquid` loads `jspdf 4.2.1` (about 350 KB) on every page. It is deferred, but only the creator needs it.
- The GemPages v6 article templates (`gem-*`) each load 7 Google Font families with every weight from 100 to 900, plus the GemPages vendor CSS and fontawesome.
- No PageSpeed or CrUX access was available, so LCP, INP and CLS are unknown.

### 9. Low: Indexing directives

- **`robots.txt.liquid`** does not exist in the live theme, so the Shopify default robots.txt and `/sitemap.xml` apply. Neither was fetched.
- **Canonical tag:** `layout/theme.liquid` outputs `<link rel="canonical" href="{{ canonical_url }}">` on every page. That is correct.
- **Pages hidden from search** (`seo.hidden = 1`): `happy-customers` (the Loox reviews page), `free-preview`, `testimonial` and `video-carousel`. Hiding the reviews page may be deliberate. The owner should confirm.
- **Unused FAQ template.** `/pages/faqs` uses the default template (suffix ""), so the repo's `page.faqs.liquid` and the theme's faqs meta fallback do not apply. Its stored meta description starts "DELIVERY AND TRACKING How long does delivery take? For pre assemble orders...".
- **how-it-works fallback never fires.** `/pages/how-it-works` uses suffix `page`, so its meta fallback is never used.
- **Two creator pages:** `/pages/create` and `/pages/editor`. Check that one canonicalizes or links to the other.
- **Sitewide schema:** Organization and WebSite JSON-LD are present. The collection page has CollectionPage plus ItemList. Neither page type has FAQPage schema, even though the product page has a visible FAQ.

## What is working

- Every non-GemPages template outputs a self-referencing canonical, a viewport meta tag, Open Graph and Twitter cards, and `html lang`.
- `article.liquid` has full BlogPosting and BreadcrumbList schema, a single H1 and a product CTA.
- The collection template has CollectionPage schema and an H1.
- Most collections and brick-bag products now have hand-written meta descriptions.
