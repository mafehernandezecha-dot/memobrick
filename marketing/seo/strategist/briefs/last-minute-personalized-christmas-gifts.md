## Search intent

Transactional with a deadline. The searcher wants a personalized gift and is worried it will not arrive. Searches peak from early December to Christmas Eve, so the page must be indexed well before December 1 (holiday rule in the page plan). Competing results are retailer listicles that often blur delivery dates. Our angle is honesty: one real order-by date (December 15, 2026), and a real option for after it (the Art Gift Kit, which they personalize later, or a gift card).

## Audience

Adults buying in December, often for a partner or parent, who left it late or could not decide. Many are on a phone. They need a clear "what can I still do today" answer.
