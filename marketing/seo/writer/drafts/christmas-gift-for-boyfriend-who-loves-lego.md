# A Christmas Gift for a Boyfriend Who Loves Building

Shopping for a Christmas gift for a boyfriend who loves LEGO-style building and already owns every box set? Give him a build that is about the two of you: a custom brick mosaic kit made from your favorite photo. Kits start at $69.88, and the order date for Christmas is December 15, 2026.

*Memobrick is not affiliated with the LEGO Group.*

## Why a brick fan wants a build of you two, not another set

A boxed set is a nice evening. But he has built it, shelved it, and the next one looks a lot like it.

A mosaic of your own photo is different. Nobody else has one. He sorts the bags, follows the map, and watches a moment you both remember appear brick by brick. It is a hobby gift and a personal gift in the same box.

## Photo ideas for him

Pick a photo that means something to him. A few ideas:

- **Your first trip together.** A beach, a mountain view, a city street you still talk about.
- **His dog or cat.** If the pet is his favorite person, make the pet the star.
- **The two of you.** A close-up with good light and nothing busy behind you works best.
- **His car, his bike, or his favorite spot.** The place where he is happiest.
- **A game night or a game-day photo.** Something that looks like him.

Choose a photo where faces and shapes are clear. Not sure which one? Upload two or three in the [editor](https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor) and compare the previews. You see the mosaic before you order. For more tips, see our [guide to personalized Christmas photo gifts](https://mymemobrick.com/blogs/news/personalized-christmas-photo-gifts).

## What the build is like

Each kit comes with 1×1 bricks in 40+ colors, sorted into small labeled bags, white baseplates, personalized building instructions (a printed color map), and a building tool. There are no big bricks and no loose guessing. You follow the map and place each small brick.

Build times are estimates and depend on the size. A 16×16 takes about 2.5 hours. A 20×20 takes about 4 hours. A 30×20 takes about 6 hours. A 40×20 takes about 8 hours. That is a quiet weekend, or a few evenings.

Give him a build nobody else can buy: a custom brick mosaic kit made from a photo of you two, his pet, or his favorite place. Kits start at $69.88.
