# Christmas Gifts for Grandparents From the Grandkids

One of the best Christmas gifts for grandparents from grandkids is a custom brick mosaic of the grandchildren, made from a photo you choose. The kids can help build it, and Grandma and Grandpa get a piece of art with their favorite faces on it.

Here is how to make it a real "from the grandkids" gift, which photo to pick, and which size fits the whole crew.

## Why grandparents love gifts the grandkids helped make

Grandparents rarely want more things. What they treasure is time with the grandkids and proof of it: the drawing on the fridge, the school photo in the wallet. A gift the kids had a hand in making lands differently from anything bought in a hurry.

A brick mosaic is a gift and a project at once. Thousands of small 1×1 bricks come together, one by one, into a portrait. Up close you see each brick. Step back and you see the grandkids smiling.

## Three ways to give a brick mosaic from the grandkids

### The kids build it before Christmas

Order the kit early, then build it together over a few December afternoons. Every kit comes with personalized building instructions (a printed color map), so even young builders can follow along with a grown-up. A 16×16 has 2,500 pieces and takes about 2.5 hours. A 20×20 has 4,096 pieces and takes about 4 hours. Split it across evenings and it becomes a family tradition before it is even wrapped.

### Build it together with Grandma and Grandpa

Wrap the kit unbuilt and open it together after Christmas dinner. Spread the bags of bricks on the table, and let everyone add a row. Grandparents and grandkids working side by side is half the gift. We share more ideas for this in [a holiday activity everyone can actually join](https://mymemobrick.com/blogs/news/a-holiday-activity-everyone-can-actually-join).

### Let the grandparents choose the photo

Not sure which photo they would pick? Give the [Art Gift Kit](https://mymemobrick.com/products/unlimited-memobrick). You buy it with no photo and receive a personalisation link by email. The grandparents choose their favorite photo, approve the design, and we make the kit and ship it straight to them. The grandkids can hand over the link in a card they decorated themselves.

## Which photo to use

You do not need a professional picture. Clear faces and good light matter more than anything. Daylight photos usually turn out better than dark indoor shots.

### All the grandkids together

The classic. One photo with every grandchild in it, from the summer cousins' trip or last Thanksgiving. Group photos need more room, so see the size section below.

### One grandchild per mosaic

If the family is spread out, each grandchild can give a small portrait of themselves. The [10×10 mini kit](https://mymemobrick.com/products/custom-bricked-mosaic-10x10) is made for one face and costs $35.00, so a set of them can become a little gallery on the grandparents' wall.

### A photo with the grandparents in it

A photo of Grandpa reading to the baby, or Grandma at the beach with the kids, often means the most. It is their memory too.

If you have two or three options, upload them in our [editor](https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor) and compare the previews before you decide. You can also ask Memobrick's designers for a [free proof](https://mymemobrick.com/pages/free-preview) before production. It is optional.
