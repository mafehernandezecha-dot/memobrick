# A Gift for the Person Who Has Everything

The best gift for someone who has everything is one they cannot already own. A custom brick mosaic built from a photo only you have is exactly that, because no one else has that moment.

If you are shopping for a parent, grandparent, partner, or that one relative who is impossible to buy for, this guide explains why a photo-based gift works and how to make one.

## Why the usual gifts fall flat

People who have everything usually have the things you can buy in a store. Another candle, scarf, or gadget joins a pile they already have. A gift card is kind, but it hands the work of choosing back to them.

What they rarely have is a piece of their own story, made just for them. That is the gap a personalized photo gift fills. For more on why this lands better, read [why personalized gifts feel so much better than generic gifts](https://mymemobrick.com/blogs/news/why-personalized-gifts-feel-so-much-better-than-generic-gifts).

## Why a photo makes the gift one of a kind

### Pick a photo with meaning

You do not need a perfect picture. Choose one with a story: a family holiday, a wedding day, a pet curled up on the couch, or the grandkids all squeezed into one frame. Clear faces and good light help. If you are unsure, upload two or three and compare the previews.

### It is something to do, not just something to open

A custom brick mosaic arrives as a kit, so the gift keeps going after the wrapping paper is gone. They can build it alone on a quiet weekend or spread the bricks out and build it with family. When it is finished, it hangs on the wall as a keepsake. We wrote about this idea in [the gift that starts a conversation](https://mymemobrick.com/blogs/news/the-gift-that-starts-a-conversation).

## How a custom brick mosaic works

### Upload, preview, choose a size

Upload your photo on our website. It creates your mosaic design and shows you a preview, so you can see your photo in bricks before you order. Then choose the size and options that suit your photo and your space.

Your DIY brick mosaic kit includes 1×1 bricks in the colors your design uses (from a palette of 40+ colors), baseplates, personalized building instructions, and a building tool. Frames are sold separately.

## FAQ

### What do you give someone who has everything?

Give them something they cannot already own. A gift made from a photo only you have, like a custom brick mosaic, is personal by definition, so it cannot duplicate anything on their shelf.

### Is a photo gift too sentimental for someone practical?

It does not have to be. A mosaic is also a build project and a piece of wall art. Practical people often enjoy the building, and the finished piece looks like art, not a scrapbook.

### Does the kit need to be built, or can it arrive assembled?

It is a build-at-home kit. You or the person you give it to places the 1×1 bricks on the baseplates using the building instructions. That is part of the fun.

### Does it come with a frame?

Frames are sold separately. You can see the options on the product page.
