# A Christmas Gift for Your Girlfriend, Built From a Photo

Looking for a Christmas gift for your girlfriend that costs less than $100 and still feels personal? Turn a favorite photo of the two of you into a custom brick mosaic kit she can build and hang. The 16×16 kit is $69.88 and the 20×20 kit is $99.88, and the order date for Christmas is December 15, 2026.

## A gift that shows you remember

Anyone can buy flowers or a candle. A photo of the two of you, built brick by brick, says you were paying attention.

The kit comes with 1×1 bricks for your design (from a palette of 40+ colors) sorted into small labeled bags, white baseplates, personalized building instructions, and a building tool. She follows the map and a photo slowly appears. Up close it is a pattern of color. From across the room, it is the two of you.

## Photo ideas that make a strong mosaic

Pick the photo you would both smile at. A few ideas:

- **Your first trip together.** A beach, a city street, a view you still talk about.
- **A night out.** A concert, a birthday dinner, a night when you both looked happy.
- **The two of you with her pet.** Many people love their pet as much as their partner. If the pet is the star, our [guide to personalized Christmas photo gifts](https://mymemobrick.com/blogs/news/personalized-christmas-photo-gifts) has more ideas by recipient.
- **A simple close-up.** Two faces, good light, nothing busy behind you.

Choose a photo where faces are close to the camera and clear. Not sure which one? Upload two or three in the [editor](https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor) and compare the previews. You see the mosaic before you order.

## Make it together or surprise her

You can wrap the kit as it comes, with a printed copy of the photo tucked inside as a hint. Then keep a quiet evening free. Put on a movie, sort the bags, and build side by side. One of you reads the map, the other places the bricks. It is a gift and a date night in one.
