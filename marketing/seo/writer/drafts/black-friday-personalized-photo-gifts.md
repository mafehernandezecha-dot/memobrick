# Personalized Black Friday Gifts Made From a Photo

Looking for personalized gifts for Black Friday that are not another doorbuster? A custom brick mosaic kit turns a favorite photo into a gift someone builds and hangs, and you can still order it in time for Christmas: order by December 15, 2026.

This guide covers why a personal gift holds up next to a deal, how to pick a size, and what to do before the sale starts.

## Why a personalized gift beats a doorbuster

Most Black Friday gifts are things anyone could buy. A personalized photo gift is the opposite. It is made from one picture that matters to one person.

A Memobrick kit arrives with everything needed: 1×1 bricks for your design (from a palette of 40+ colors) sorted into small labeled bags, white baseplates, personalized building instructions, and a building tool. The person you give it to follows the map and watches the photo appear, then displays it. That makes it a gift and an activity in one box.

It suits a partner, parents, grandparents, a best friend, or the person who has everything. Browse more by recipient in our guide to [personalized Christmas photo gifts](https://mymemobrick.com/blogs/news/personalized-christmas-photo-gifts).

## Choose the photo before the sale starts

The photo is the part that takes time, so pick it now and the sale day is easy.

- Choose one clear subject that fills most of the picture, in good light.
- Faces should be sharp and close to the camera.
- Avoid busy backgrounds and heavy filters.

Upload two or three in the [editor](https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=seo&utm_campaign=black-friday-gifts#editor) and compare the previews. You see the mosaic before you order.

If you would rather have help, Memobrick's designers can create the design for you and send a [free proof](https://mymemobrick.com/pages/free-preview) before production. It is optional.

Cannot decide on a photo at all? The [Art Gift Kit](https://mymemobrick.com/products/unlimited-memobrick) lets you buy now with no photo and send a personalization link, so the person you are gifting picks their own. Gift cards are also available.

Yes, if you plan the photo first. A personalized photo gift is made for one person, so it does not compete with generic deals, and you have time to order before December 15, 2026.

### Can a personalized gift still arrive before Christmas?

Order by December 15, 2026 to receive your kit before Christmas. Kits are made and shipped from Miami, Florida.

No. Codes cannot be combined with each other, with other offers, or with free shipping. Free shipping is for US orders of $125 or more.

### Which size is the best value?
