# Photo to Brick Mosaic Maker: Upload, Preview, Build

The Memobrick editor is a photo to brick mosaic maker: upload a photo and see it as a custom brick mosaic before you order. Pick a size, order a DIY brick mosaic kit, and build it at home with 1×1 bricks.

[Make yours from your photo](https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor)

## How it works in 4 steps

### 1. Upload your photo

Open the editor and upload a photo from your phone or computer. Clear, well-lit photos with the subject large in the frame work best. For quick tips, see our guide to the [best photo for a brick mosaic](https://mymemobrick.com/blogs/news/best-photo-for-a-brick-mosaic).

### 2. Adjust colors and details

The editor turns your photo into a grid of 1×1 bricks in 40+ colors. You can adjust colors and details until the mosaic looks right to you.

### 3. Preview sizes and pick one

Compare sizes before you order. The smallest kit is 16×16 inches with 2,500 pieces, from $69.88. The largest is 40×50 inches with 20,480 pieces, from $431.98. In between you will find 20×20 (4,096 pieces, $99.88), 30×30 (9,216 pieces, $209.99) and more. Our [brick mosaic size guide](https://mymemobrick.com/blogs/news/brick-mosaic-sizes) shows every size.

### 4. Order a kit and build at home

Your kit arrives ready to build. Follow the printed color map, open the bags one color at a time, and press the bricks onto the baseplates.

## Want help instead?

If you would rather not use the editor, Memobrick's designers can create the design for you and send a [free proof](https://mymemobrick.com/pages/free-preview) before production. It is optional. You can always make your own design in the editor.

## What the kit includes

- 1×1 bricks in small bags sorted by color
- White baseplates
- A printed color map to follow
- The building tool

Frames are sold separately. It is a build-at-home kit, not an assembled piece.

## How long does it take to build?

Shopify lists about 2.5 hours for the 16×16 and 4 hours for the 20×20. Bigger sizes take longer, up to about 20 hours for the 40×50. These are estimates, and your time may differ. Building together makes it faster. See the [size guide](https://mymemobrick.com/blogs/news/brick-mosaic-sizes) for each size.

## FAQ

### Can I see the mosaic before I pay?

Yes. The editor shows your photo as a mosaic before you order, and you can adjust colors and details.

### What photo should I upload?

A sharp, well-lit photo with the subject large in the frame. See our [photo tips](https://mymemobrick.com/blogs/news/best-photo-for-a-brick-mosaic).

### Is the preview what I will build?

The preview is the design your kit is made from. Bricks are small squares, so up close you see each brick and from a few steps back you see your photo.

### Can Memobrick design it for me?

Yes, if you prefer. Memobrick's designers can create the design and send a free proof before production. It is optional.

### Is this LEGO?

No. Memobrick makes custom brick mosaic kits with 1×1 bricks. Memobrick is not affiliated with the LEGO Group.
