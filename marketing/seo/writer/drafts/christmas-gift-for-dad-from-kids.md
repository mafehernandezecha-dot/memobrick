# A Christmas Gift for Dad From the Kids, Built From a Photo

Looking for a Christmas gift for dad from the kids that is not another mug? Turn a favorite photo into a custom brick mosaic kit he can build and hang. Kits start at $69.88, and the order date for Christmas is December 15, 2026.

## Why a gift you help make beats one off a shelf

Dad says he wants nothing. Then he opens a tie and says thanks. What he keeps is the gift that has you in it.

A photo mosaic is made from your picture, so no one else has the same one. And it is not only something to unwrap. He gets an evening of building, then a piece of art that stays on the wall. Up close it is a pattern of 1×1 bricks in 40+ colors. From across the room, it is the photo.

## Photo ideas of the kids with dad

Pick a photo that already makes him smile. A few ideas:

- **The kids with dad.** A beach day, a ballgame, a messy kitchen. Faces close to the camera and clearly lit work best.
- **His car, his boat or his fishing trip.** The thing he talks about on the weekend, with a kid or two in the frame.
- **The family dog.** Many dads pretend they are not dog people. The photo says otherwise.
- **A photo from when he was young.** Ask grandma for an old print and take a clear picture of it. A tidy, well lit copy makes a better mosaic than a faded one.

You see the mosaic before you order. If you want more ideas by recipient, our [guide to personalized Christmas photo gifts](https://mymemobrick.com/blogs/news/personalized-christmas-photo-gifts) covers the whole list.

## How kids can take part

This works best as a family project, with an adult helping.

- **Pick the photo.** Let the kids vote on it. Even young children can point at their favorite.
- **Help with the building.** The kit has small 1×1 bricks, so an adult should supervise. Kids can sort bags and place bricks while an adult reads the map.
- **Wrap it.** Add a drawing from the kids on the box.

Two ways to give it. Wrap the kit as it comes, and build it with him on Christmas week. Or build it first and give him the finished piece, ready to hang. Building time is an estimate for the whole kit, so plan it as one or two evenings, not a rush.
