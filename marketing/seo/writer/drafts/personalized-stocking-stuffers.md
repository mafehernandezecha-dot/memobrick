# Personalized Stocking Stuffers Made From a Photo

Looking for personalized stocking stuffers that are small, personal, and more than a trinket? A one-face 10×10 brick mosaic kit made from a favorite photo is $35.00 and gives them something to build on Christmas afternoon. To receive it before Christmas, order by December 15, 2026.

## Why a small photo kit works as a stocking stuffer

Most stocking gifts get opened, smiled at, and forgotten by New Year's. A small kit made from one face is different in three ways:

- **It is personal.** It uses a photo of someone they love, or their own.
- **It is something to do.** The mosaic is built by hand from 1×1 bricks, so the gift keeps going after the paper is on the floor.
- **It ends up on display.** A finished 10×10 sits on a shelf or a desk.

## Four personalized stocking stuffers from Memobrick

### 1. The one-face mini kit (10×10, $35.00)

The [10×10 one-face kit](https://mymemobrick.com/products/custom-bricked-mosaic-10x10) turns one face into a mosaic. The product page lists 1,024 1×1 bricks on 1 white baseplate and a build time of about 1 hour (an estimate). Like every Memobrick kit, it comes with the bricks sorted into small labeled bags by color, personalized building instructions, and the building tool. Frames are sold separately.

Best photo: a close-up of one face in good light. If you want help choosing, the [photo tips](https://mymemobrick.com/blogs/news/brick-mosaic-sizes) in our size guide cover what works at each size.

### 2. The Art Gift Kit, if you do not have the photo

Not sure which photo to use, or want them to choose? The [Art Gift Kit](https://mymemobrick.com/products/unlimited-memobrick) lets you buy now and personalize later. The 10×10 is $35.00. How it works:

1. You buy the kit without a photo.
2. We send a personalization email.
3. The recipient picks the photo and approves the design.
4. We make the kit and ship it to them.

Write the details on a card and put the card in the stocking.

### 3. A Memobrick gift card

A [gift card](https://mymemobrick.com/products/memo-brick-gift-ard) comes in $10, $25, $50, $100, or $166 amounts. It is the easiest stocking stuffer for someone whose taste you cannot guess, and it works after the deadline below too.

### 4. A ready-made 10×10 art kit ($30.00)

If they like the building more than the photo, try a ready-made 10×10 art kit such as [Happy Bee](https://mymemobrick.com/products/bee) or [Evil Eye](https://mymemobrick.com/products/eye-on-you), $30.00 each. These are not personalized.
