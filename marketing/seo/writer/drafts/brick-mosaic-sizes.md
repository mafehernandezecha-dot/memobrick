# Which Brick Mosaic Size Should You Choose?

Brick mosaic sizes run from 16×16 to 40×50 inches, with 2,500 to 20,480 pieces and an estimated 2.5 to 20 hours to build. Pick the size by the space on your wall, the time you want to spend building, and who will be in the photo.

Below you will find what is in every kit, a size-by-size table, and a simple way to match a size to the gift. For current prices, see each size on the product page.

## How long does it take to build?

### Build times are estimates, so split them across evenings

A 9-hour mosaic does not need one long day. Most people build a section at a time, a baseplate or two per evening, and bring the pieces together at the end. Bricks come sorted in labeled bags by color, and the printed color map shows you where each one goes.

### A good size for a first build

The 16×16 (2,500 pieces, about 2.5 hours) and the 20×20 (4,096 pieces, about 4 hours) fit in a single weekend afternoon. They are a kind way to start if you have not built a brick mosaic before.

### A family project

From 30×30 up, the build becomes a shared activity. Spread the bags on a table and let everyone take a baseplate. The finished piece is something the whole family can point to.

## FAQ

Start with the photo. One or two faces suit a smaller size. A group suits a larger one. Then think about wall space and how long you want to build. Upload your photo and use the preview to compare.

### How long does it take to build?

Estimates run from about 2.5 hours for a 16×16 to about 20 hours for a 40×50. You can split the build across several evenings.
