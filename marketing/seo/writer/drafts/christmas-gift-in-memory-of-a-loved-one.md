# A Christmas Gift in Memory of a Loved One

A Christmas gift in memory of a loved one can be as simple as a favorite photo, turned into something the family builds together and keeps. A custom brick mosaic does that: you pick a photo where they look like themselves, build it brick by brick over the holidays, and give it a place on the wall.

The first Christmas without someone is hard. The table looks different, and the stories come out at odd moments. This guide is for anyone looking for a memorial Christmas gift, for their own family or for a friend who is grieving. It covers whether the gift is right, how to choose the photo, and what to expect.

## Is a memorial gift right for them?

Some people want to see their loved one's face at Christmas. Others are not ready yet. Both are normal.

If you are buying for someone else, ask gently first. You can say something like "I have a photo of your dad I love. Would you like something made from it, or is it too soon?" Their answer tells you more than any gift guide.

If they are not sure, there is a softer route. Give them the choice of the photo, or the choice to wait, with the Art Gift Kit (more on that below).

## Choosing the photo

The photo matters more than the size. Take your time with it.

### A photo where they look like themselves

Choose the picture people would recognize at a glance. Their usual smile, their glasses, the hat they always wore. A face that fills much of the frame and is evenly lit works best. Busy backgrounds and tiny faces lose detail when they become bricks.

### Old prints and scans

Many of the best photos of a parent or grandparent exist only on paper. Lay the print flat in soft daylight and take a clear, straight photo of it, or scan it.

Then upload it to the [editor](https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?size=20x20&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=seo&utm_campaign=memorial-christmas#editor) and look at the preview. You see the mosaic before you order, so you can judge it yourself. If you would rather have help, Memobrick's designers can create the design from your photo and send a [free proof](https://mymemobrick.com/pages/free-preview) before production. It is optional.

### Let the family choose

Sometimes the right photo is not yours to pick. With the [Art Gift Kit](https://mymemobrick.com/products/unlimited-memobrick), you buy the kit now with no photo. We email you a personalization link to send to them whenever the time feels right. They choose the photo and approve the design, and we make the kit and ship it to them.

## Building it together over the holidays

This is what makes it different from an ornament or a frame. A mosaic kit gives the family something to do with their hands on a quiet evening.

The kit comes with white baseplates, small 1×1 bricks in 40+ colors sorted into labeled bags, a printed color map, and a building tool. One person reads the map, another finds the colors, someone else places the bricks. Slowly, the face appears.

People often talk while they build. Who took this photo. Where they were that day. What they would have said about the whole project. There is no right way to do it, and no need to finish in one night.

## FAQ

### What is a meaningful Christmas gift in memory of someone?

Something made from them, not only about them. A favorite photo turned into a custom brick mosaic gives the family an evening of building together and a piece to keep on the wall.

### Can I use an old or printed photo?

Yes, you can try. Take a clear, straight photo of the print in soft light or scan it, then preview it in the editor. If you would like help, our designers can create the design and send a free proof before production.

### Is a memorial gift appropriate for someone grieving?

It depends on the person. Some find comfort in seeing a loved one's face, others are not ready. Ask gently first, or give the Art Gift Kit so they can choose the photo and the timing.

### Can the family pick the photo themselves?

Yes. With the Art Gift Kit you buy the kit with no photo, and they receive a link to choose the photo and approve the design. We then make the kit and ship it to them.

### Does it come with a frame, and when do I need to order for Christmas?

Frames are sold separately. Order by December 15, 2026 to receive your kit before Christmas.
