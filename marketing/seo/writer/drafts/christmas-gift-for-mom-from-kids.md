# A Christmas Gift for Mom From the Kids, in Bricks

Looking for a Christmas gift for mom from the kids that is not another mug? Turn a favorite family photo into a custom brick mosaic kit, and let the kids help pick it, build it, and wrap it. Order by December 15, 2026 to have it for Christmas.

## Why a gift you help make beats one you buy off a shelf

Moms keep the things that took effort. A drawing on the fridge, a card with crooked letters, a photo in a frame. A brick mosaic is in that same family, with a bigger result: a portrait made of thousands of small 1×1 bricks that goes on the wall.

The kids are not just the givers. They get to choose the photo, see it turn into a mosaic on screen, and, with an adult helping, place bricks themselves.

## Photo ideas that work

- **The kids together.** A clear, close photo of the children, faces in good light.
- **Mom with the kids.** The one she always says she is not in enough.
- **Her pet.** If the dog is practically a fourth kid, put the dog in.
- **Then and now.** A childhood photo of mom next to a recent family photo. Upload both in the editor and compare previews.

The best photos have one clear subject that fills the picture. Busy backgrounds and dark rooms turn out less clearly. You can see the mosaic before you order, so try two or three. Want a second opinion first? The [free preview](https://mymemobrick.com/pages/free-preview) page explains how to get a proof.

## How kids can take part

1. **Pick the photo.** Let the kids vote on it.
2. **Preview it.** Upload it in the [editor](https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor) and watch it become a mosaic.
3. **Build it.** The kit comes with the 1×1 bricks for your design (40+ colors, sorted into small labeled bags), white baseplates, a personalized building map, and a building tool. An adult should help with the small pieces.
4. **Wrap it.** Wrap the finished piece, or wrap the kit with the photo tucked inside and build it together on Christmas week.

Build times are estimates, so think in evenings, not minutes. A 20×20 has 4,096 pieces and takes about 4 hours. A 30×20 has 6,144 pieces and takes about 6 hours. A larger one is a good project for the whole family over the holidays.

## FAQ

### What can kids give mom that is not store-bought?

Something made from a photo only your family has. A custom brick mosaic lets the kids choose the picture and help build it, so the gift has their hands in it.

### Can a child help make it?

Yes. Building is the activity, placing small 1×1 bricks on a baseplate by following the map. An adult should supervise because the pieces are small.

### Which photo works best?

One with a clear subject, good light, and faces that fill a good part of the frame. Upload a few in the editor and compare the previews before you order.

### How long does it take? Does a frame come with it?

Build times are estimates: about 4 hours for a 20×20, about 6 hours for a 30×20, about 8 hours for a 40×20. Frames are sold separately.

### When do I need to order for Christmas?

Order by December 15, 2026 to receive your kit before Christmas.
