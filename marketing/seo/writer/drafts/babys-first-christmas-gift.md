# Baby's First Christmas Gift: A Photo Keepsake to Build

Looking for a baby's first Christmas gift that is more than another ornament? Turn a favorite baby photo into a custom brick mosaic kit that the family builds and hangs on the wall.

It marks the year in a way a tiny flat ornament cannot, and the new parents do not have to pick anything out. Order by **December 15, 2026** to receive your kit before Christmas.

## Why a photo keepsake fits a baby's first Christmas

Babies change every week. A photo from this first December is one you will want in a year, and in ten.

A brick mosaic turns that photo into something you can see from across the room. Up close it is a pattern of small 1×1 bricks in 40+ colors. Step back and it is the baby's face. The kit arrives with everything needed: bricks for your design sorted into small labeled bags, white baseplates, personalized building instructions, and a building tool.

It is also a gift with a second step. Once the baby is asleep, the adults can build it together, a few rows at a time.

## Which baby photo to use

You do not need a studio portrait. You need a clear photo in good light.

### A close-up of the baby's face

A bright, close photo with the face filling most of the frame makes the strongest mosaic. For a small gift, the [10×10 one-face mini kit](https://mymemobrick.com/products/custom-bricked-mosaic-10x10) is $35.00 (1,024 bricks, 1 baseplate, about 1 hour to build). For a wall piece, the [16×16 kit](https://mymemobrick.com/products/1-big-board) is $69.88 (about 2.5 hours).

### Baby with mom, dad or the grandparents

A photo with two people needs more room. Look at the 20×20 ($99.88, about 4 hours) or larger in the [size guide](https://mymemobrick.com/blogs/news/brick-mosaic-sizes), and check the preview before you order.

### Not sure? Let the parents choose

If you cannot pick a photo, give the [Art Gift Kit](https://mymemobrick.com/products/unlimited-memobrick). You buy it now with no photo and the parents receive a personalization link by email. They choose the photo and approve the design, and we make the kit and ship it to them.

Want to compare photos first? Upload two or three in the [editor](https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?size=16x16&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=seo&utm_campaign=baby-first-christmas#editor) and look at the previews. You see the mosaic before you order.

## Where it goes

Hang it on the nursery wall or set it on a shelf. Frames are sold separately: $49.00 for a [16×16 frame](https://mymemobrick.com/products/frame-big-board) and $65.00 for a 20×20 frame at the time of writing.

## A safety note

The kit is made of small bricks. It is for adults to build, so keep the pieces away from babies and young children, and from anyone who may put small pieces in their mouth. Once the mosaic is built and framed, it is wall art.
