# Custom Brick Mosaic Kit From Your Photo: What You Get

A custom brick mosaic kit turns your photo into a box of 1×1 bricks in 40+ colors, white baseplates, a printed color map made from your design, and a building tool. You build it at home, one small brick at a time, until your photo appears on the wall.

[Make yours from your photo](https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?size=20x20&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=seo&utm_campaign=diy-kit#editor)

## What is in the box

Every Memobrick kit is made from the design you create in our editor. Inside the white box you will find:

- **1×1 bricks** in the colors your design needs, from a palette of 40+ colors.
- **Small labeled bags.** The bricks come sorted by color, and each bag shows a color name and number.
- **White baseplates.** One for a 16×16, four for a 20×20, nine for a 30×30. Larger sizes join several baseplates edge to edge.
- **Personalized building instructions,** a printed color map of your own design.
- **A building tool** to help place and remove bricks.

A frame is not included. Frames are sold separately (see Frame and display below).

## How the color map works

Your color map is your photo, turned into a grid. Each square on the map is one brick, and each color on the map matches one of the labeled bags.

1. Open one bag at a time, or set out a few colors in small bowls.
2. Start with one baseplate. Find the matching section on the map.
3. Place each brick where the map shows its color, row by row.
4. When a baseplate is done, move to the next one.

Up close you see every brick. From a few steps back, you see your photo. That moment, when the faces show up, is the best part.

## How hard is it?

There is no cutting, gluing or painting. You follow the map and press each brick onto the baseplate. Build at your own pace: a few rows with a coffee, or a long session with music on. Bigger sizes have more pieces, so they make a longer project.

The pieces are small. If children are helping, an adult should be there.

## Who it suits

- **A personal gift.** A photo of the two of you, the kids, or the dog, made into something they build and keep.
- **A couple's night in.** One person reads the map, the other places the bricks.
