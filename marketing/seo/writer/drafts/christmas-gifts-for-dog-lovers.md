# Christmas Gifts for Dog Lovers: Their Dog in Bricks

The best Christmas gift for a dog lover is one that is about their dog. A custom brick mosaic kit made from a photo of their dog starts at $35.00, and they build it themselves from 1×1 bricks. To receive it before Christmas, order by December 15, 2026.

## Why dog lovers love a gift of their own dog

Most dog gifts are about dogs in general: a mug, a stocking, a bowl with a name on it. A mosaic of their own dog is different. It has the face they know, the ear that never lies flat, the look they give you at dinner.

- **It is personal.** Nobody else has this one.
- **It is something to do.** The kit comes with the bricks sorted into small labeled bags by color, personalized building instructions, and the building tool. They build it at home.
- **It stays.** A finished mosaic hangs on a wall or sits on a shelf, long after the wrapping is gone.

## How to get the right photo of their dog

### Where to find one without spoiling the surprise

You do not need to ask them. Try these:

- Their social media, if they post their dog (most dog owners do).
- A family member or a friend who has photos from walks and visits.
- Your own phone, from the last time you saw the dog.

### What makes a dog photo work

- The face fills most of the picture.
- Both eyes are clear and open.
- The photo was taken at the dog's eye level, not from above.
- The background is simple.
- The light is bright and even.

You can see how a photo turns into a mosaic before you order. If you would like help choosing, Memobrick's designers can create the design and send a [free proof](https://mymemobrick.com/pages/free-preview) first. It is optional.

## Gift ideas for every dog lover

### For the dog mom or dog dad

The easy win. Pick the photo they would choose themselves: the one with the goofy face. A 16×16 or 20×20 hangs well in a hallway or an office.

### From the dog

Let the family give it "from" the dog. Add a card in the dog's voice ("I made you something. Mostly I supervised."). It makes the gift part of the joke and part of the home.

### For a family with two dogs

Bigger sizes give more room for more subjects in one photo. Check the preview in the editor to see how your photo looks at each size before you order.

### For someone who lost their dog this year

Handle this one gently. A mosaic of a dog who is gone can be a comfort, and it is a very personal choice, so only give it if you know the person would welcome it. Our article on [remembering a pet with a brick mosaic](https://mymemobrick.com/blogs/news/blog-memorial) may help you decide.
