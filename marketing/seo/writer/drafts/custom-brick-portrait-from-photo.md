# Custom LEGO-Style Portrait From Your Photo

A custom brick portrait turns a photo of you, your partner, your family or your dog into a mosaic of small 1×1 bricks that you build yourself. Up close you see every brick; from across the room you see the face.

You upload the photo, see the mosaic before you order, and pick a size. Then a kit arrives with everything you need to build it. [Make yours from your photo](https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=seo&utm_campaign=brick-portrait#editor).

## What makes a good portrait photo

A brick portrait is a pixel portrait, so the photo does most of the work. A few things help a lot:

- **Get close.** The face should fill much of the frame. A small face in a wide landscape loses its features when it becomes bricks.
- **Face the camera.** Front-facing or a slight turn works best. Strong profiles and sunglasses hide the eyes, and the eyes are what people recognize.
- **Use soft, even light.** Daylight from a window is ideal. Harsh shadows across half the face turn into dark patches.
- **Keep the background simple.** A plain wall or a blurred background lets the face stand out.

Old prints work too. Lay the print flat in soft light and take a straight photo of it, or scan it, then check the preview.

## Portrait of one person, a couple, a family or a pet

The subject decides the size more than anything else.

### One person

A single face suits the smaller sizes. A [16×16](https://mymemobrick.com/products/1-big-board) or [20×20](https://mymemobrick.com/products/2-2-boards) is a good place to start for a head-and-shoulders portrait. It is also a quick build and fits on a shelf or a desk.

### Couples and families

More people need more bricks, so each face keeps its detail. Shopify lists how many people fit a photo for these sizes:

- [30×40](https://mymemobrick.com/products/3-4-boards): 1 to 5 people
- [40×50](https://mymemobrick.com/products/4-5-boards): 1 to 6 people

### Pets

Dogs and cats make great brick portraits. Fur has many shades, and the eyes carry the whole picture, so a close photo at eye level works best. ## Why 40+ colors matter for skin tones and eyes

A face is mostly small changes in tone: the shadow under the cheek, the light on the forehead, the color of the eyes. With only a few colors, those changes flatten out. With 40+ colors, the mosaic can follow them more closely, so skin tones look warmer and eyes stand out.

In the editor you can adjust colors and details and compare sizes before you decide. Be honest with yourself about what you see: a brick portrait is a pixel mosaic, not a photo print. It looks most like the photo from a few steps back. The preview is the best guide, so judge it there before you order.

## A portrait you build together

A brick portrait is also something to do. The build takes from about 2.5 hours for a 16×16 to about 20 hours for a 40×50, so it can be one quiet evening or a project for several weekends.

It works well as a shared activity. One person reads the color map, another finds the bricks, someone else places them. Row by row, the face appears. When it is finished, it goes on the wall or a shelf as a keepsake you made yourselves.

## FAQ

### Can you make a portrait of a person from any photo?

Most clear photos work. Choose one where the face is close, facing the camera and evenly lit, with a simple background. Upload it to the editor and check the preview before you order.

### Will a brick portrait look like me?

It is a pixel mosaic made of 1×1 bricks, so it reads best from a few steps away. How close it looks depends on the photo and the size. You see the preview first, so you can judge it yourself before ordering.

### How many people fit in a brick portrait?

Shopify lists 1 to 3 people for the 40×20, 1 to 4 for the 30×30, 1 to 5 for the 30×40 and 1 to 6 for the 40×50. For one face, the 16×16 or 20×20 is a good start.

### Can your designers make the portrait for me?

Yes, if you prefer help. Memobrick's designers can create the design from your photo and send a free proof before production. It is optional; you can also make it yourself in the editor.

### Is a Memobrick LEGO-style portrait made by LEGO?

No. It is a custom brick mosaic made of 1×1 bricks. LEGO is a trademark of the LEGO Group, and Memobrick is not affiliated with the LEGO Group.
