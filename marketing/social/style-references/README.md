# Style references

AI images the owner made and likes. Hugo matches this look for AI image posts by describing it in the prompt. Do not pass these files to the image tool: each shows a mosaic, and Gemini copies that mosaic's subject. Pass only `brick-texture-closeup.jpg` (bricks up close, from owner footage) for the brick look, and add text afterwards with `marketing/tools/add_text.py`.

| File | Use it for | What makes it work |
|---|---|---|

| couple-building-table.webp | Building-experience posts | Close, candid moment placing bricks, bricks sorted in small tubs |
| family-holding-mosaic.webp | Family posts | Everyone in the photo holding their own mosaic, smiling at the camera |

Shared look: warm natural light, cream and soft wood interiors, real-looking people in casual clothes, the mosaic large and sharp in the frame, soft pink, navy and pastel accents, short warm headlines.

Not used as references (examples the owner shared that break the rules): shelves of another brand's building sets and minifigures in the background, bigger 2×4 bricks floating around, a "returning customers" offer that is not in the offers calendar, and a "free proof" promise.
