## New replies for gift questions (paste only when someone asks)

Use only in groups where business replies are allowed. Change the first words to fit the question. No prices or codes. Never reply twice in the same thread. Add the link only if the group allows links.

**1. "Gift for someone who loves their dog or cat?"**
A picture of the pet itself is hard to beat. One idea: turn a favorite photo of the pet into a brick mosaic you build yourself, a few rows at a time. Full disclosure, I run Memobrick, which makes these, so I am biased. You can see your pet's photo as a mosaic before deciding anything: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/dog-brick-mosaic?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=community-20261104&utm_content=reply-pet

**2. "Gift for a parent or in-law who has everything?"**
Something with the family in it tends to beat something new. A photo of the grandkids or a family trip can become a brick mosaic they hang on the wall, and building it together can be the gift too. I run Memobrick, which makes these, so take that with a grain of salt. The preview is free and shows the mosaic before you choose anything: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=community-20261104&utm_content=reply-parent

**3. "Quiet gift ideas for a kid who likes to build?"**
Anything with a clear picture at the end keeps older kids going. A brick mosaic from a favorite photo works like that: color by color until the picture appears. I run Memobrick, which makes them. Bricks are small pieces, so keep them away from toddlers.
