**R1. "Which size do I need for a family photo?" or "Can a photo with several people work?"**
It depends on how many faces are in the picture. The bigger the group, the bigger the mosaic you want, so everyone's face has enough bricks to look like them. The sizes list how many people each one suits, from about 1 to 3 people up to 1 to 6 for the largest. Fewer people, closer in the frame, always reads best. Full disclosure, I run Memobrick, which makes these, so I look at a lot of group photos. You can try yours and see how it comes out before deciding anything: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=community-20261107&utm_content=reply-groupsize

**R2. "I want to make something from an old photo of someone I lost. Is that okay?" (reply only in a group that is not a grief support group, or where the poster asked for ideas)**
I'm sorry for your loss. Many people do turn a favorite photo of someone they love into something to keep. If you only have a printed photo, a sharp picture of it taken in daylight, without glare, is a good place to start. I run Memobrick, which makes brick mosaic kits from photos, so I am biased, but you can see how a photo looks as a mosaic before you decide anything, and if you would like help with the design, our designers can make it and send a free proof first: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=community-20261107&utm_content=reply-memory
