Hi everyone. I'm Eden, and I run Memobrick, a small company that turns photos into brick mosaic kits. I'm sharing a free printable we made, in case it's useful to anyone here.

It's called the Calm Build Plan. It's one page with six simple steps for a quiet building session: pick a quiet spot, decide how long you'll build, open one color bag at a time, finish one row at a time, choose a clear stopping point, and look back at what you built.

We made it because a mosaic is very predictable to build. Every brick is the same size, a color map shows exactly where each one goes, and the picture appears row by row. Some people like to build alone, and some like to build together with jobs: one person reads the map, one finds the colors, one places the bricks.

It's a free download, no email needed: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/calm-build-gift?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=community-20261028

If you try the plan, I'd love to hear what you would change.
