# Memobrick Builders: Eden's 10-minute setup and first 3 prompts (Patricia, 2026-10-29)

Plan approved as A-2026W44-043 (rules, welcome post and 14 prompts are in `own-group.md`). This file is only the steps and the first 3 posts, ready to paste. Do it on a computer or phone, signed in as Eden with admin rights on the Memobrick Page.

## Setup, 10 minutes

1. Open Facebook, tap Menu, Groups, Create new group.
2. Group name: Memobrick Builders.
3. Privacy: Public. (Posts can be found by people who haven't joined yet.)
4. Create it, then open the group's settings.
5. Description (paste): A friendly group for people building custom brick mosaics from their own photos. Share your progress, get photo tips, and show off your finished mosaic.
6. Cover photo: the 16x16 Bernese mountain dog next to its photo, from https://mymemobrick.com/products/1-big-board (first product photo).
7. Membership approval: turn on "admins approve members". Add the two questions: "Have you built a Memobrick mosaic, or are you planning one?" and "Do you agree to the group rules?"
8. Rules: add the 7 rules from `own-group.md` one by one (Settings, Rules).
9. Link it to the Memobrick Page: Page settings, Linked accounts / Groups, add Memobrick Builders.
10. Post the welcome post from `own-group.md`, then pin it (three dots, Pin post). Before pasting, you may remove the "Order by December 15" and free-proof lines if you want the group to stay free of selling, as in your rule for other groups. Your call.
11. Post prompt 1 below today, prompt 2 tomorrow, prompt 3 the day after. Invite only people who already know Memobrick (your own friends, family, past customers who wrote to you). Don't add anyone without asking, and don't invite people from other groups.

## First 3 prompts, paste as Eden

Prompt 1 (day 1, introductions):
Welcome to Memobrick Builders! Tell us your name, where you're building from, and the photo you turned into a mosaic (or want to). A pet, the kids, a wedding photo, anything goes. I'm Eden, and I'll read every reply.

Prompt 2 (day 2, photo tips, add the dog photo above if you like):
What makes a photo turn into a great mosaic? What I've seen so far: a close-up, the face turned to the camera, good light, and a background that isn't the same color as the hair or fur. What would you add? And if you have a photo you're unsure about, post it here and we'll look at it together.

Prompt 3 (day 3, which photo should I brick?):
Torn between two photos? Post both and let the group vote. Tell us who the mosaic is for and where it will hang. Everyone is welcome to vote, even if you haven't built one yet.
