## Part 1. Replies to the 5 most common buyer questions

These come from `customer-questions.md` (every question there has a count of 1 so far, so I picked the five a gift buyer asks first). No prices, codes or deadlines. The editor link carries utm_medium=group so Shopify shows which group sent the visit. Keep the link only where the group allows links; otherwise delete the last sentence and answer in words. Always say you run Memobrick, in your own words.

**Q1. "What photo works best for a brick mosaic?" (or "Does a group photo work?")**
Close-ups win. A face large in the frame, good light from a window, and a plain background. Busy backgrounds and tiny faces get lost when the picture becomes bricks. A group photo can work, but the bigger the group, the bigger the mosaic you want. Full disclosure, I run Memobrick, so I look at a lot of these. You can try any photo and see it as a mosaic before deciding anything: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=community-20261106&utm_content=reply-bestphoto

**Q2. "Will it actually look like the person?" / "What comes in the box?"**
Up close you see every brick, and from across the room you see the photo. The kit comes with small labeled bags of 1×1 bricks sorted by color, white baseplates, a printed color map that shows where each brick goes, and a tool. I run Memobrick, so I am biased, but you do not have to take my word for it. You can see your own photo as a mosaic first, free: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=community-20261106&utm_content=reply-looklike
(If they would rather have help with the design, say Memobrick's designers can make it and send a free proof first. Optional, never required.)

**Q3. "Does it come framed?"**
The kit is for building at home and the frame is a separate option, so you can build it and then choose how to hang it. I run Memobrick, which makes these. If it helps, the preview is free: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=community-20261106&utm_content=reply-frame
(Do not promise a frame for a specific size. Some frames are limited. Send them to the site for the size they want.)

**Q4. "Is this LEGO?" / "Is it like those AI brick filters?"**
Not LEGO, and Memobrick is not affiliated with LEGO. A filter gives you a picture on a screen. This one is a physical kit: your photo becomes a map, and you build it yourself from small 1×1 bricks, a few rows at a time. Full disclosure, I run Memobrick, so I like the second kind. You can preview your photo as a mosaic here: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=community-20261106&utm_content=reply-notlego

**Q5. "Can the kids or grandkids help make it?" / "Is it a gift they can build together?"**
Yes, for older kids with a grown-up. Give everyone a job: one reads the map, one finds the color bag, one places bricks, and leave it on the table for a week so it slowly turns into the picture. The bricks are small, so keep them away from toddlers and pets. I run Memobrick, which makes these, so take that with a grain of salt. The preview is free: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=community-20261106&utm_content=reply-kidshelp

Quick game for the weekend: how many colors is your dog? 🐕

You will say "brown" or "black and white". Now look closer. A black lab is black, charcoal, a touch of brown where the light hits, and white on the chin. A golden is cream, honey, caramel and a little orange. When you rebuild a dog out of tiny bricks, every one of those shades needs its own color.
