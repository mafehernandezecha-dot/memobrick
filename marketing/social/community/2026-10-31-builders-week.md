# Memobrick Builders: prompts for the week of Nov 2 (Patricia, 2026-10-31)

Use these after the group is open and prompts 1 to 3 in `own-group-setup-steps.md` have gone out. If the group is not created yet, hold these; creating it takes about 10 minutes. Post as Eden. No prices, codes or deadlines in the group.

Monday Nov 2, which photo should I brick?
Pick a photo you love and ask the group: "Would this one make a good mosaic?" Post it, tell us who it is for, and we will share what we see. Tips that help: a close-up, the face toward the camera, good light, and a background that is not the same color as the hair or fur. Not sure which of two photos to use? Post both.

Wednesday Nov 4, build tip.
A tip from the people who build these: open one color bag at a time and finish a row before you move on. The picture appears row by row, and it is easy to stop and come back later. What is your tip for building? Share how you set up your table, your music, or your snack.

Friday Nov 6, progress photos.
Show us where your mosaic is today. Half built, one corner done, or just the bags laid out on the table all count. Tell us how many rows you have finished or how long you have been at it. If it is finished, post it with the photo it came from.

Rules reminder: members' photos stay in the group unless they say yes in writing to being shared elsewhere.
