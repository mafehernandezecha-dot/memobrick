## Reply set for questions we keep seeing (paste only when someone asks)

These come from the questions we have logged (`shared/customer-questions.md`). No comment or message questions have been reported to me this week, so these are the likeliest ones. Each is written to be pasted as a reply; change the first words to match the question. No prices or codes. Add the link only if the group allows links.

**1. "Is this LEGO?"**
It is not LEGO and we are not affiliated with LEGO. It is a brick mosaic kit made of small 1×1 bricks, with a printed color map, and it is built on white baseplates. If you want to see your own photo as a mosaic first, the preview is free: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=community-20261103&utm_content=reply-lego

**2. "What photo works best?"**
Close-ups win. Get the face large in the frame, with even light and a background that is a different color from the hair or fur. Group shots taken from far away lose the faces. You can upload a photo and see how it looks before you decide anything.

**3. "How long does it take to build?"**
It depends on the size. The smallest one, 16×16 inches, is 2,500 bricks and takes about 2.5 hours. Bigger ones join several baseplates, so they take longer. Many people build a few rows at a time over a week.

**4. "Does it come with a frame?"**
The kit has the bricks, the baseplates, the printed color map and the tool. Frames are sold separately, so you can pick one or hang it your own way.

**5. "Is it safe for kids?"**
The bricks are small, so it is a build for older kids and adults, or for families building together. Keep them away from babies and toddlers who put things in their mouths.

**6. "Can I see it before I buy?"**
Yes. Upload a photo in the editor and you see it as a mosaic first. If you would rather have help, our designers can make it and send a free proof, and that is optional.

**7. "What do I give someone who has everything?"**
A photo of a moment they love, turned into something they build or hang. A pet, a family trip, grandparents with the kids. It is personal, so it does not duplicate anything they already own. Memobrick makes these as kits from your own photo.
