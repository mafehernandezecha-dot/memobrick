## Reply set (paste only when someone asks)

No new questions came from groups this week (none reported). These five come from the logged buyer questions in `marketing/shared/customer-questions.md`, with couples/anniversary and grandparents first. No prices, codes or deadlines. Add a link only if the group allows it.

**1. "Anniversary gift idea that is not jewelry or dinner?"**
One idea: a brick mosaic of a photo from a trip or a favorite day. It is a kit, so you build it together over a few evenings and it ends up on the wall. I'm Eden from Memobrick, happy to share how it works if you'd like.

**2. "What do I give grandparents who have everything?"**
Something about the grandkids rather than another thing. A photo of the kids together, turned into a brick mosaic they can hang. The kids can help find colors and place bricks. Bricks are small, so keep little ones supervised. I'm Eden from Memobrick, one idea among many.

**3. "Will a group photo work?"**
It can, but faces get small. Close-ups with one to three people, faces toward the camera and a plain background, turn out best. You can see the design as a mosaic before you decide, so try your photo and see.

**4. "Can I make one from an old printed photo of someone we lost?"**
Many people do, with a phone picture or scan of the print, as long as the face is clear. It is a gentle thing to build slowly. If you want help, Memobrick's designers can make the design and send a free proof first. It is optional.

**5. "I'm not good with design, can someone do it for me?"**
Yes, you can upload your photo in the editor and see the mosaic yourself, or ask Memobrick's designers to create it and send a free proof before anything is made. The proof is optional.
