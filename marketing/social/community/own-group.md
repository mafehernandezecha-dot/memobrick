# Memobrick Builders: plan for Memobrick's own Facebook group

Proposed 2026-10-28 by Patricia (social-community-manager). For Eden to approve once. After that I prepare 3 prompts a week.

## Why

A group of people who already build (or want to build) photo mosaics gives us a place to answer "which photo should I use?" questions, show real finished builds (with permission), and remind members of true dates such as December 15 for Christmas. Members who see other people's finished mosaics are more likely to start their own.

## Setup (Eden, by hand)

- Name: **Memobrick Builders**
- Created from the Memobrick Facebook Page, so the Page is the admin.
- Privacy: public (anyone can see posts, so they help new visitors), members approved by an admin.
- Membership questions: 1) "Have you built a Memobrick mosaic, or are you planning one?" 2) "Do you agree to the group rules?"
- Short description: "A friendly group for people building custom brick mosaics from their own photos. Share your progress, get photo tips, and show off your finished mosaic."
- Cover photo: a real product photo (the 16×16 Bernese mountain dog next to its photo).
- Link it from the Memobrick Facebook Page and, later, from the order thank-you page (site change, needs its own approval).

## Group rules

1. **Be kind.** Every build and every photo is someone's memory. No mean comments.
2. **Share your own photos only.** Post builds you made, or ask permission before sharing someone else's.
3. **No selling or ads from members.** This group is for building, not for promoting other shops.
4. **Questions welcome.** Photo choice, colors, missing bricks: ask. For an order problem, message Memobrick directly so we can help you privately; please don't post order numbers or addresses here.
5. **Small parts.** Kits have many small bricks. Keep them away from young children and pets.
6. **No medical claims.** Many members build to relax. Please share your experience, but no advice about treatments or therapy.
7. **Memobrick may share your posts.** We will always ask first before using your photo anywhere outside this group.

## Welcome post (pin it)

Welcome to Memobrick Builders! 👋

I'm Eden, and I run Memobrick. We turn your photos into DIY brick mosaic kits, and this group is the place to share the building part: the first rows, the "wait, which color is this?" moments, and the finished mosaic on the wall.

Here's how to start:
1. Introduce yourself and tell us what you're building (or what photo you're thinking of).
2. Share progress photos anytime. We love halfway pictures.
3. Ask questions. Photo choice, size, colors: someone here has been there.

Planning a gift? Order by December 15 to receive it before Christmas. Not sure your photo will work? Our designers can make the design and send you a free proof first: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/free-preview

Building to unwind? We made a free Calm Build Plan you can print: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/calm-build-gift

Please read the group rules, and have fun building!

## First 2 weeks of prompts

Start the day the group opens. One post a day; Eden can skip any day. Weekly regulars: Monday "Which photo should I brick?", Wednesday build tip, Friday progress, last Friday of the month "Show your mosaic".

| Day | Type | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intro | Welcome! Tell us your name, where you're building from, and the photo you turned (or want to turn) into a mosaic. |
| 2 | Photo tips | What makes a photo turn into a great mosaic? Our tips: close-up, face to the camera, good light, plain background. What would you add? |
| 3 | Which photo should I brick? | Torn between two photos? Post both and let the group vote. |
| 4 | Calm building | How do you like to build: music, a show, a podcast, or total quiet? Share your setup. (Free Calm Build Plan: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/calm-build-gift) |
| 5 | Progress Friday | Show us where you are right now, even if it's just three rows. |
| 6 | Pets | Pet Weekend: share a photo of the pet you built, or the one you'd love to build. |
| 7 | Build together | Who builds with you? Kids, a partner, grandparents? One reads the map, one finds the colors, one places the bricks. How do you split the jobs? |
| 8 | Which photo should I brick? | Family photo, couple photo, or the dog? Post your options for a Christmas mosaic and we'll help you pick. Reminder: order by December 15 for Christmas. |
| 9 | Build tip | Sorting tip: open one or two color bags at a time into small bowls. How do you sort your bricks? |
| 10 | Question | What's the hardest color to tell apart in your kit? Let's help each other. |
| 11 | Gift stories | Did you give a mosaic as a gift? Tell us who it was for and how they reacted (only your own story, please). |
| 12 | Progress Friday | Finished a baseplate this week? Show it off. |
| 13 | Display | Where did your finished mosaic end up? Wall, shelf, frame, office? Post a photo. |
| 14 | Show your mosaic | First "Show your mosaic" day! Post your finished build. With your permission, we may feature some on the Memobrick Page. |

Notes:
- Only Eden posts as Memobrick. No fake member accounts, no posts pretending to be a customer.
- Feature a member's photo outside the group only after they say yes in writing; record it in `marketing/conversion/reviews-ugc/approved-stories.md`.
- Offers: mention a code only if `offers-calendar.md` shows it active that day (EARLYHOLIDAY20 until Nov 22; no code Nov 23 to 26; BRICKFRIDAY25 Nov 27 to 29; CYBERBRICK20 Nov 30), and say it can't be combined with other offers or free shipping.
