# Memobrick Builders: prompts for the week of Nov 16 (Patricia, 2026-11-05)

Use after the group is open. Post as Eden. No prices, codes or deadlines in the group.

Monday Nov 16, which room?
Post a photo of a wall or shelf and ask: where would you hang a mosaic of your favorite photo, and which photo would it be? Let members answer in comments. No selling, just ideas.

Wednesday Nov 18, build rhythm.
Ask: how do you build, a few rows each evening, a long weekend, or with someone else? Share what works for you. A mosaic of small 1x1 bricks goes at your own pace.

Friday Nov 20, thankful photo.
Ask members to name the one photo they are most thankful to have, without posting it. What makes it the one? Good for people still choosing which photo to turn into a mosaic.

Rules reminder: members' photos stay in the group unless they say yes in writing to being shared elsewhere.
