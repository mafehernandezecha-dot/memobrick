# Memobrick Builders: prompts for the week of Nov 9 (Patricia, 2026-11-02)

Use after the group is open. Post as Eden. No prices, codes or deadlines in the group.

Monday Nov 9, caption this build.
Post a close-up of a mosaic in progress (ours or one you imagine) and ask: what do you see in it already? Members guess who or what it will be before the faces appear. Good for people who have not started yet.

Wednesday Nov 11, photo tips.
Two photos, same person: one far away in dim light, one close with window light. Ask members which will make the better mosaic and why. Close-ups with the face large in the frame and a plain background win.

Friday Nov 13, build setup.
Show us your build spot: the table, the lamp, the color bags laid out. Tell us your system, such as one bag at a time or all bags open. Small bricks, so tell us how you keep them away from little ones and pets.

Rules reminder: members' photos stay in the group unless they say yes in writing to being shared elsewhere.
