**R1. "Is a photo gift too personal for my partner's parents?" / "What do I give my in-laws that isn't boring?"**
A photo gift can work with in-laws if the photo is a happy, easy one: a family gathering, a holiday, the grandkids, their dog. I would skip anything that feels like a private moment, and skip photos where someone in the family may not love how they look. A picture they can hang in a hallway or on a shelf is usually safer than something they have to use. I run Memobrick, which makes brick mosaic kits from photos, so I am biased. If you want to see how a photo looks as a mosaic before deciding anything, the preview is free to try (open it on your phone): https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=community-20261110&utm_content=reply-inlaws

**R2. "Is a brick mosaic safe to give for a baby?" / "Gift for a baby's first Christmas?"**
Not as a toy. The bricks are small pieces, so a baby should not handle them, and a kit like this is for an older child or an adult to build. What works is a keepsake the grown-ups make: a baby photo built as a brick mosaic and hung on the wall or placed on a high shelf, out of reach. I run Memobrick, so take that with a grain of salt, and please keep any small bricks away from babies and pets. If you want to look at how a baby photo turns into a mosaic, open this on your phone: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=community-20261110&utm_content=reply-baby
