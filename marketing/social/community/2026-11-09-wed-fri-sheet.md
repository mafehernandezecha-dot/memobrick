**R1. "How do I get a good photo of my dog for a gift without them knowing?" / "I want a portrait of someone's pet as a surprise."**
Take the photo when they are relaxed, not posing: near a window with daylight, at the pet's eye level, with the face filling most of the picture and a plain background behind it. A few quick shots in burst mode usually give you one sharp one. If the owner has a favorite spot or a funny angle, that makes it feel like their pet. I run Memobrick, which makes brick mosaic kits from photos, so take that with a grain of salt. You can see how a photo looks as a mosaic before deciding anything: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=community-20261109&utm_content=reply-petphoto

**R2. "Is this the same as an AI LEGO filter?" / "Is it a real kit or just a picture?"**
It is a real kit, not a filter. You see your photo as a mosaic preview on the website, then a box arrives with baseplates, small 1×1 bricks sorted by color, and a map, and you build the picture yourself. It is not LEGO brand and not affiliated with LEGO. I run Memobrick, so I am biased, but the preview is free to try: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=community-20261109&utm_content=reply-notafilter
