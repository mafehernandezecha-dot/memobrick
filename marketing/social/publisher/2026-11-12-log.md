## Kit rules from now on (for every reel and carousel scheduled in Buffer)
1. Audio: every reel's audio must be AAC-LC, 48 kHz, stereo, 128 kbps. In add-music.sh change `-ar 44100` to `-ar 48000` before the next reel (one-line change, not made today because this task was read only); for an existing music file, re-encode just the audio: `ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 128k -ar 48000 -ac 2 -movflags +faststart out.mp4`, and check with `ffprobe -select_streams a -show_entries stream=sample_rate`.
2. Video format: H.264 High, yuv420p, 1080x1920, 30 fps, keyframe at least every 5 s, faststart, 3 to 90 s long, under 10 MB (all current reels are 15 to 20 s and 3.5 to 6.3 MB, so length and size were not the cause).
3. Never swap media inside a scheduled Buffer post: if a file changes, upload it to Shopify under a new name (no `?v=` URL) and create the post again after deleting the old one.
4. Never replace a Shopify file in place for a scheduled post; a changed slide gets a new file name.
5. Facebook reels: read the post back 1 hour after its time; if Facebook fails again with a 48 kHz file, that reel goes to the Mac kit (Facebook section without a Buffer line) instead of retrying.
