## Brick Me patterns used (from the ads analyst's capture)

#2 push-in reveal (finished mosaic first, then into the bricks) · #3 and #8 gift unboxing · #4 family unboxing · #5 on the wall · #6 and #7 building together, gift for family and pet parents · #9 pet slow pan · #10 testimonial. Common rules: real people and product, a face or bricks in the first second, no price or code, the offer is a free design or preview, and one shared primary text with only the video changing.

Our angles with evidence (`audience-insights.md`): family photo as the gift (best-selling ad, 8 purchases), pets and dogs (cheapest purchases, $25.45 on the dog video), couples and "for him" (best CTR, 2.08% to 2.25%). Real-product short video is the only format with purchases under $40. Avoid surprise-reaction framing that doesn't show the product in the first second ("surprice husband": 0.91% CTR, 0 purchases).

## Shared ad text (all ads)

Primary text:

> Turn your favorite photo into a custom brick mosaic you build yourself.
> Upload your photo, see it as a mosaic, and choose your size. Your kit comes with 1×1 bricks sorted by color, baseplates, a printed color map and the building tool.
> A personalized photo gift for couples, families and pet lovers. As seen on FOX.
> See your photo in bricks with a free photo preview.

- Headline: "See your photo in bricks". Description: "As seen on FOX".
- Link: `https://mymemobrick.com/pages/free-preview?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=2026-11-brickme-style&utm_content=<ad id, e.g. a1-look-closer>` (the page answers 200 today).
- Button: Shop now (what Brick Me runs). Learn more is the alternative to test, since the page is a preview and not a product.
- No price, code, deadline, "LEGO" wording or health claim. Every fact is from `product-facts.md` and the brand skill. On-screen captions describe what happens. None is a customer quote.

## A3 pet with voice-over (2026-11-02, after Eden: "make it more human, best practices for the text")

There is one edit per computer voice in `voice/` (881, 886, 564), each in 4:5 and 9:16: `A3-pet-vo<voice>-4x5.mp4` and `-9x16.mp4`, with a `-sheet.png` showing one frame per phrase. They are built by `brickme-style-ads-2026-11-01/vo_cuts.py` from `voice/vo_cues.json`. They use the same cleared footage and real product only, with no interpolation and no warping. Draft only.

- **Length:** voice + 0.5 s (13.4 to 13.7 s). Every shot starts on its phrase:
  - "is that her dog?": the woman hugging the dog.
  - "In bricks?!": a white flash to the framed mosaic.
  - "Yep. Real bricks.": a macro of a finger pressing a brick.
  - "one photo": the printed color map of her dog's photo.
  - "We sent the kit": the color-sorted bags.
  - "She built it": hands on the board.
  - "next to the real one": the wall, then the real dog.
  - "Want to see yours?": the mosaic close.
  - Last phrase: a bright end card.
- **Captions:** the exact caption text from the cue file. Each shows from its phrase's start until the next phrase starts, one at a time. They are on a white card, 76 px, with the key word in brand coral ("dog?", "bricks?!", "Real bricks.", "one photo", "kit", "built", "real one", "yours?"). Wrapping is balanced so no word sits alone on a line.
  - The hook caption is on screen from frame 1.
  - In 4:5 the card sits in the upper middle. In 9:16 it sits between 14% and 35% of the height, clear of the Reels UI. The end-card text ends above 65%.
- **Audio:**
  - Voice: high-passed at 90 Hz and lightly compressed (3:1).
  - Music (original, generated): 20% under the voice, about 14 to 15 dB below it. It rises to 45% when the end card starts while the last phrase is still playing, and goes to full after the last word.
  - Mix: -14.1 to -14.4 LUFS, sample peaks -1.4 to -1.5 dBFS, no clipping.
