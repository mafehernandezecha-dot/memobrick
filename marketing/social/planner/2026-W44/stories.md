# Stories, 2026-W44 (Oct 26 to Nov 1)

Story ideas for Instagram and Facebook Stories, posted by hand. Not in the calendar. Every Story uses a sticker. Never send DMs to followers yourself or in bulk; only reply to people who write to us first.

The weekly "Send us your photo" repeat is Story 1. When someone sends a photo, save it to the "submitted photos" Drive folder (the owner creates it and writes its ID under `## Submitted photos` in `marketing/social/footage-rights.md`), and send them the permission message in this week's run report before using it anywhere. A photo with a child also needs a parent's or guardian's written yes.
