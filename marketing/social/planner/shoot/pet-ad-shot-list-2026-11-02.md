# Pet ad: shot list for a new phone shoot (2026-11-02)

Why: Eden said "the footage need better direction and focus". Current cuts reuse second-generation clips (re-encoded reels, cropped and zoomed), so several shots are soft: the finger-on-brick macro, the bag shot, and the whole 9:16 version.

## Camera setup (every shot)
- Phone back camera, 4K 30 fps, vertical (9:16). We crop the 4:5 from it.
- Clean the lens. Lock focus and exposure: press and hold on the subject until "AE/AF Lock" shows.
- Daylight from a window at the side, no overhead light, no flash. Plain background (white wall or wood table).
- Hold still or use a stand or a stack of books. No digital zoom: move the phone closer instead.
- 5 to 8 seconds per shot, start and stop with a still second. Film each shot 2 or 3 times.
- No music while filming, no other brands, logos or TV in frame.

## Shots (in order of the script)
1. **Hook (person + pet + mosaic):** someone holds the pet next to the finished, framed mosaic, both faces to camera, then turns to look at the mosaic and smiles. Phone at chest height.
2. **The mosaic alone:** framed mosaic on the wall, straight on, slow walk-in from 1.5 m to 50 cm.
3. **Macro "placed by hand":** 15 to 20 cm from the board, focus locked on one stud, a finger presses one 1×1 brick in. Side window light so studs show texture.
4. **"Your favorite photo":** a phone screen showing the pet's real photo, held next to the printed color map. Screen brightness up.
5. **"The kit, sorted by color":** overhead, open box on a table, hands lay out 5 or 6 color bags in a neat row so the labels read.
6. **"You build it at home":** overhead, hands filling a row of bricks on the baseplate following the map. Then 10 s from the side at table height.
7. **Timelapse:** phone on a stand overhead, Timelapse mode, the whole build or 20 to 30 minutes of it.
8. **"On your wall":** hang the frame, step back, pet walks in or is held next to it.
9. **End card frame:** the finished framed mosaic straight on, 3 seconds, perfectly still.
