# Daily engagement list, 2026-10-28 to 2026-11-04 (Instagram follower sprint)

For Eden, by hand, about 15 minutes a day. Written by the social planner (Hugo) on Wednesday 2026-10-28 for the sprint in `marketing/social/planner/sprint-2026-10-29/sprint.md`.

## Rules (read every day)

- Comment genuinely, about the post in front of you. No copy-paste comments: the ideas below are starting points, always add a detail you can see in that post (the dog's name, the color of the room, what the kids are building).
- Follow an account only if you truly like it. Never unfollow later to gain follows back.
- No selling and no links in comments on other people's posts. Don't mention Memobrick unless they ask.
- Stop for the day when it stops feeling natural.
- Never send mass or automated DMs. No follow and unfollow tactics, ever.

## The 15 minutes

1. **3 minutes: our own comments and DMs first.** Reply to every comment on our posts from the last 24 hours (answers within an hour help a post reach more people). Today there are 4 older comments to answer if you haven't: 2 on "What actually comes in a Memobrick kit?" (Oct 23), 1 on "The best part of this gift happens before it is wrapped" (Oct 24), 1 on "What do you give the parents who say they don't need anything?" (Oct 25). I can't read comment text from here (Buffer gives counts only), so the reply ideas are below by kind.
2. **9 minutes: 10 to 15 small real accounts** from the day's hashtags (below). Open "Recent", pick people (not brands) who posted in the last 3 days, live in the US, have a normal following (a few hundred to a few thousand, real-looking comments, no "DM to promote"). Like the post you comment on, leave one specific comment, and follow only if you'd genuinely enjoy their posts. Skip competitors (other photo mosaic or custom portrait shops), coupon pages and accounts that look bought. Write each handle in the table at the bottom (14-day rule).
3. **3 minutes: 3 bigger niche posts** from the day's list, one thoughtful comment each, early (within the first hour of their post if you can).
