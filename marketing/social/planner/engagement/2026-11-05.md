# Daily engagement list, 2026-11-05 to 2026-11-11

For Eden, by hand, about 15 minutes a day. Written by the social planner on Thursday 2026-11-05. It covers the days until my next run (Thursday Nov 12) and adds new hashtags and themes to `engagement/2026-11-03.md` and `engagement/2026-10-29.md` (same routine; reply ideas and the log are at the bottom of the Oct 29 list). Do not repeat an account you used in the last 14 days.

## Rules (read every day)

- Comment genuinely, about the post in front of you. No copy-paste comments: the ideas below are starting points, always add a detail you can see in that post.
- Follow an account only if you truly like it. Never unfollow later to gain follows back.
- No selling and no links in comments on other people's posts. Do not mention Memobrick unless they ask.
- Stop for the day when it stops feeling natural.
- Never send mass or automated DMs. No follow and unfollow tactics, ever.

## What I cannot do from here

I cannot open Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or Pinterest (no login), so I cannot check that a small account is real, active and not bought, and I will not invent handles. Each day gives hashtags and what to look for; you pick 10 to 15 real people on the spot (not brands, not competitors, not coupon pages, normal following with real-looking comments, posted in the last 3 days) and write the handles in the log at the bottom of the Oct 29 list.

## Each day

1. 3 minutes: reply to every comment and DM on our own posts from the last 24 hours (reply ideas by kind are at the bottom of the Oct 29 list; facts only from `product-facts.md`). I have no new comment data (no snapshot), so there are no suggested replies to specific comments this time.
2. 9 minutes: 10 to 15 small real accounts from the day's hashtags.
3. 3 minutes: 1 to 2 bigger niche posts.
