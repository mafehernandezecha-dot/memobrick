# Daily engagement list, 2026-10-29 to 2026-11-05

For Eden, by hand, about 15 minutes a day. Written by the social planner on Thursday 2026-10-29. It adds to the sprint list `engagement/2026-10-28.md` (same 15-minute routine) and uses different hashtags and accounts from it.

## Rules (read every day)

- Comment genuinely, about the post in front of you. No copy-paste comments: the ideas below are starting points, always add a detail you can see in that post.
- Follow an account only if you truly like it. Never unfollow later to gain follows back.
- No selling and no links in comments on other people's posts. Do not mention Memobrick unless they ask.
- Stop for the day when it stops feeling natural.
- Never send mass or automated DMs. No follow and unfollow tactics, ever.

## What I cannot do from here (please read)

I cannot open Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or Pinterest from this environment (no login; the Meta connector has no Instagram account linked, and Buffer shows counts, not comment text). So I cannot check that a small account is real, active and not bought, and I will not invent handles. For the small accounts, each day gives hashtags and what to look for; you pick 10 to 15 real people on the spot and write the handles in the log at the bottom (so none repeats within 14 days). The bigger accounts below are well-known public accounts; open each one's newest post before using the comment idea.

## Each day

1. **3 minutes: our own comments and DMs first.** Reply to every comment from the last 24 hours. The 4 comments on our carousels (Oct 23, 24, 25) are the only ones Buffer shows; reply ideas by kind are at the bottom.
2. **9 minutes: 10 to 15 small real accounts** from the day's hashtags. Pick people (not brands) who posted in the last 3 days, live in the US, have a normal following (a few hundred to a few thousand, real-looking comments), like the post and leave one specific comment. Skip competitors (photo mosaic and custom portrait shops), coupon pages and accounts that look bought.
3. **3 minutes: 1 to 2 bigger niche posts** from the day's list.
