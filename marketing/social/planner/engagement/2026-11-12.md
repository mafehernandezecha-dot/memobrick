# Daily engagement list, 2026-11-12 to 2026-11-18

For Eden, by hand, about 15 minutes a day. Written by the social planner on Thursday 2026-11-12. It covers the days until my next run (Thursday Nov 19). Same routine as `engagement/2026-10-29.md` (reply ideas by kind and the log are at the bottom of that list). Do not repeat an account you used in the last 14 days.

## Rules (read every day)

- Comment genuinely, about the post in front of you. No copy-paste comments: the ideas below are starting points, always add a detail you can see in that post.
- Follow an account only if you truly like it. Never unfollow later to gain follows back.
- No selling and no links in comments on other people's posts. Do not mention Memobrick unless they ask.
- Stop for the day when it stops feeling natural.
- Never send mass or automated DMs. No follow and unfollow tactics, ever.

## Day by day

### Thu Nov 12: Christmas gift planners
- Hashtags: #christmasgiftideas, #giftsforher, #christmasshopping, #giftguide2026
