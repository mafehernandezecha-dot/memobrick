## Hook and why it should work

Hook line: "We brick your pet. Episode 1" over a real macro of fingers holding a single 1×1 brick (slow motion). Bricks up close in the first second (the audience loves the 1×1 bricks), a pet (dog video was our cheapest purchase in Meta ads at $25.45, `audience-insights.md`), and a numbered series people follow to see the next episode. The end asks for their pet's photo, which turns viewers into senders; tagged senders share the episode with their own followers.
