## 1. More Reels (2 extra, both ready)

Reels are the format that reaches non-followers, so the sprint adds two, at noon so they do not compete with the 19:00 posts. Both open on 1×1 bricks in the first second (our best reel did; people leave at about 3 s), and both ask for something people want to answer.

- **Thu Oct 29 12:00, S-20261029-2 "We brick your pet", Episode 1** (16.5 s). Real macro of fingers holding a 1×1 brick, then a dachshund photo turning row by row into its MemoBrick Studio mosaic, a macro zoom-out, and "Want your pet next? DM us their photo." Starts the weekly series.
- **Fri Oct 30 12:00, S-20261030-2 "Same photo, 3 sizes"** (19 s). One couple photo as 16×16, 20×20 and 30×30 Studio mosaics, then "Which one would you hang? Comment 16, 20 or 30." Comment bait that also answers the size question.

Both go to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest (video pin) and YouTube Shorts with per-channel UTMs. Every mosaic is a MemoBrick Studio export (`Studio:` line in each post file). AI photos: the AI label goes on.

## 2. "We brick your pet" weekly series (Send us your photo)

- Every Thursday, one episode: a follower's pet photo turned into a Studio mosaic reel, tagged to the sender. Tagged people share it to their Stories, which brings their followers to us.
- Episode 1 (Oct 29) is our own example dog, because no follower has sent a photo with permission yet.
- How a photo gets in: someone DMs a photo. Eden saves it to a "submitted photos" Drive folder (please create it and put its ID under `## Submitted photos` in `marketing/social/footage-rights.md`), adds a row as `to confirm`, and replies with the message below. Only when they reply "yes" (and a parent or guardian, if a child is in the photo) does Eden set the row to `ok`. Then the planner makes the episode in the Studio.
- Never send DMs to people who have not written to us first, and never in bulk.

Permission message (Eden sends it by hand, only to people who sent us a photo):

> Thank you so much for sending us your photo! We'd love to turn it into a brick mosaic video for our Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Pinterest, and we'll tag you. Is it OK for Memobrick to use your photo in our posts? Just reply "yes" to give permission. If the photo shows a child, please confirm you're their parent or guardian. You can ask us to take it down any time.

When nobody has sent a photo with permission by Tuesday, that week's episode becomes "Guess the pet" with an AI pet photo (labeled), and the call to send photos repeats.
