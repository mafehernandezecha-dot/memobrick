## Hook and why it should work

Hook line: "Same photo. 3 sizes." over a macro of the 30×30 mosaic's studs, zooming out. "Which size?" is a real buying question (size guide carousel S-20261029-1 answers it in text; this shows it). Couples have our best ad CTR (MEN ads 2.08% to 2.25%, `audience-insights.md`). Asking "Comment 16, 20 or 30" invites one-word comments, and comments push a reel to more non-followers. It shows honestly that bigger sizes hold more detail, which helps people pick the size that suits their photo.
