## Hook and why it should work

Hook line: "Same photo. 3 sizes." over real footage of this couple's finished brick mosaic lying on a table, bricks filling the screen from the first frame. "Which size?" is a real buying question (size guide carousel S-20261029-1 answers it in text; this shows it). Couples have our best ad CTR (MEN ads 2.08% to 2.25%, `audience-insights.md`). Asking "Comment 16, 20 or 30" invites one-word comments, and comments push a reel to more non-followers. It shows honestly that bigger sizes hold more detail. The real build and the finished piece held up make it believable, which the AI version was not.
