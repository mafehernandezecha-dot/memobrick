# Proposed weekly follower goals

Proposed by the social planner on 2026-10-14. If approved, nothing automatic happens: the planner copies these goals into `## Goals` in `marketing/social/planner/followers.md` with the approval ID and reports against them every week.

## Why goals are written as a percentage

Current follower counts and the average weekly change are **missing** for every platform: `marketing/data/` has no snapshots yet and the Meta connector has no Instagram account linked to the ad account. So the goals below are growth rates, not fixed numbers. The first Thursday run after the snapshots arrive turns each rate into a weekly number (for example, 1,000 followers at +1.5% is +15 a week) and writes it next to the rate.

| Platform | Current followers | Average weekly change so far | Proposed weekly goal | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | missing | missing | +1.5% a week | 7 posts and 3 Stories a week, a running series ("Guess the photo Friday"), the "Send us your photo" campaign and the daily engagement list are new; a steady account posting daily usually grows 1% to 2% a week. Instagram brings fewer orders than Facebook (6 vs 20 in 90 days), so growth here must be buyers, not volume. |
| Facebook | missing | missing | +0.5% a week | Facebook brings the most social orders (20 in 90 days, $2,251) but Pages grow slowly from organic posts; the goal is modest and the real measure stays orders from `utm_source=facebook`. |
| TikTok | missing | missing | +3% a week | Reels and photo carousels go up daily; TikTok reach does not depend on existing followers, so small accounts can grow faster in percentage terms. |
| Pinterest | missing | missing | +2% a week, and track monthly views and outbound clicks too | Pins keep bringing visitors for months, and outbound clicks to the site matter more than followers. Needs a Pinterest business account first (owner to confirm it exists). |

## Rules that stay in place

- Followers are a means; orders are the goal. Every goal is reviewed next to orders from social UTMs.
- No bought followers, likes or views, no follow and unfollow, no engagement pods, no bots or automation.
- New goals are proposed only if the numbers are far off these for 4 weeks running.
