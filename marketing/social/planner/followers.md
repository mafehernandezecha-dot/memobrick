**What the numbers say (small sample, about 1,100 people reached in 8 days):**

1. **Reels reach, carousels engage.** Reels averaged 168 reach against 90 for carousels (1.9× more), so reels are how new people find us. Carousels averaged 8.6% engagement against 2.3% for reels and got all 4 comments: people who see a carousel stop and read.
2. **Nobody shared and nobody followed.** 0 shares and 0 follows on every post. No post yet gives people a reason to send it to someone (a tag, a "which one" question, their own pet) or to come back (a numbered series, a promise of the next episode).
3. **People leave in about 3 seconds.** Average watch 3.05 s on 15 to 38 s reels. The best reel (4.09 s, 321 reach) opened on real hands pressing 1×1 bricks. The longest (38 s family reel) held people the shortest (2.17 s). The first second decides the reach.
4. **Saves come from reveals.** Both saves were reels that end on a reveal (guess the photo, family photo).
5. **Evening reels did best.** The three 19:00 ET reels reached 321, 195 and 160; the noon reel reached 118. One noon post is not enough to rule noon out.

**What the sprint changes (Oct 29 to Nov 4):** two extra reels that open on bricks in the first second and ask for a comment or a photo (shares and follows), a numbered weekly series ("We brick your pet"), daily Stories with stickers, a profile tune-up so profile visits turn into follows, and Eden's 15 minutes a day of hand engagement.
