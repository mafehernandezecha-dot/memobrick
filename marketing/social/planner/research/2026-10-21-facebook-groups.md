# Facebook groups research: where Memobrick's buyers gather

Run: 2026-10-21, social planner, at Eden's request (chat, 2026-10-21). Research only: nothing was joined, posted, commented or messaged.

## Read this first: how sure are these numbers?

- **Facebook could not be opened from the agent's sandbox.** The network policy blocks facebook.com, and also the directory and blog pages (Hive Index, Bricks McGee, Crafts & Creatives and others). Everything below comes from **web search results**: Facebook's own indexed page snippets, and third-party directories and blog lists quoted in those results.
- So **every member count is "per <source>, <date>"**, and almost every rule is **not verified**. Facebook hides most group rules from people who are not members, and search engines index few of them.
- I found no reliable "posts per day" figure for any group. Activity is **not verified** for all of them.
- Where two sources disagree, both numbers are shown. Nothing was rounded up or guessed.
- **Because of this, the 2-week test starts with a read-only check** (step 0 in the test plan): Eden, or the Mac's Claude in Chrome while logged in as Eden, opens each test group and records its privacy, member count, posts per day, rules, and whether Pages may join. Nothing is posted until a group's rules are written down in `marketing/social/planner/research/facebook-groups-rules.md`.

## What the buyer data says (why these categories)

From `marketing/outreach/gift-guide-pr/buyer-profile.md`, `marketing/social/planner/audience-insights.md` and `marketing/shared/offers-calendar.md`:

- Buyer: a US woman aged 35 to 64 (mostly 35 to 54) buying a personalized photo gift for her partner, kids, parents or pet. Average order $97; comfort zone $70 to $150.
- Christmas (Nov and Dec) is 44% of the year's orders. Demand doubles the week of Nov 10. Order-by date for Christmas: December 15, 2026 (verified).
- Facebook already brings about 3 times the orders Instagram does (90 days: Facebook 20 orders, $2,251; Instagram 6 orders, $620).
- Pets are the cheapest purchases in ads (dog video, $25.45 a purchase); family and couples carry the volume.
- **Not our buyer:** people looking for LEGO toys, free templates, or the LEGO hobby itself. Those searches spent about $172 in 28 days with 0 purchases. So LEGO adult-fan groups score low on fit even though the product is made of bricks.

## How each group was scored

Each group gets three scores from 1 to 5, then an overall score where fit counts double: Overall = (2 x Fit + Size + Openness) / 4.

- **Fit:** how close the members are to our buyer (women 35 to 64, US, gift buyers, parents, grandparents, pet owners).
- **Size and activity:** member count from the best source found. Activity is unknown for all, so this is size only.
- **Openness:** how likely the group is to accept a member's honest post about a product she makes. Because rules are not verified, this is an **estimate (est.)** from the group's type: big hobby and pet groups almost always ban self-promotion in the main feed; craft groups usually allow sharing a finished project; grief groups must never get product posts.

### Gift ideas, Christmas gift ideas, couples, personalized and handmade buyers

- **No real gift-idea, Christmas-gift or couples group was found.** Every "Christmas gift ideas" and "Christmas lovers" result was a Facebook **Page** (for example Christmas lovers, about 250K likes; The Magic of Christmas, about 138K followers; All Things Christmas, about 43,308 likes), and many are old (2016). Pages cannot be posted in like groups.
- **Handmade and "shop small" promotion groups exist but are full of sellers, not buyers.** Their promo threads ("Self-Promo Saturday", weekly promo threads) are where members post offers, but the readers are mostly other sellers. Low value for orders, and posting the same link in many of them is exactly the spam pattern to avoid. Not recommended.
- Gift-idea **questions** do happen inside mom and pet groups ("what should I get my mom for Christmas?"). Answering those honestly, when the group allows it, is the best gift-idea use of Facebook groups.

## Recommended 5 for the 2-week test

Chosen to cover our three best buyer themes (dogs, cats, family photos and crafts) with a mix of very large and small, close-knit groups:

1. **The Dog Mom Club (national)** (dogs, women 35 to 64)
2. **Golden Doodle Owners** (one breed, devoted owners)
3. **Cat Lovers Group Official** (cats, very large)
4. **Craft & Creative Ideas DIY** (public craft group, the building experience)
5. **Scrapbooking, Cards, & Crafts** (family photo keepers)

If step 0 shows a group bans member business posts outright, or Pages and personal posts about one's own products, swap it for the next bench group with the same theme: Forever Golden Retriever or Dog Lover for dogs, Kids' Crafts and Activities or DIY Crafts & Projects for crafts.

## How Memobrick takes part honestly

### Who posts

- **Most groups only take personal profiles.** Some let Pages join and post as the Page, if the admin turns that on. Step 0 records which.
- Where Pages are allowed, **post as the Memobrick Page**: it is honest by default, and Page followers grow.
- Where only people can post, **Eden posts from her personal profile and says who she is in every post**: "I run Memobrick, a small business that makes these kits." US rules on endorsements (the FTC) require that a seller says so when she talks about her own product. Never pose as a customer, never use a second or fake account, never ask friends to post "I found this great shop".
- One person, one account, per group. Eden joins only the 5 test groups, answers the joining questions truthfully, and reads each group's rules before her first post.

### Value first

- **About 4 to 1.** For every post that shows Memobrick, Eden makes about 4 helpful contributions in that group: a kind comment on someone's pet photo, a real answer to a craft question, a gift idea that is not ours.
- **The first week is listening only.** Eden reads the group, comments where she has something real to say, and learns what does well there. No Memobrick post in week 1.
- **Product posts are project posts.** Show the build and the story (photo, color map, hands placing 1×1 bricks, finished piece). No prices, codes or links in the post unless the rules allow them.
- **Never AI images in groups.** Only real photos and videos from the cleared footage (`marketing/social/footage-rights.md`, status `ok`) or a mosaic Eden built herself. Group members expect real things, and an AI image there would read as a customer photo.

### Rotation

- **At most one Memobrick post per group in the 2 weeks**, and at most one group post per day across all groups.
- **Never the same text or photo in two groups.** Each group gets its own post written for its topic and rules (dogs get a dog mosaic, crafters get the build process, scrapbookers get the photo story).
- **Never the same link in many groups in a day.** Facebook's spam systems flag one account sharing one link across groups, and that can limit the account.
- Plan (week 2 of the test): Mon Dog Mom Club, Tue Craft & Creative Ideas DIY, Wed Golden Doodle Owners, Thu Scrapbooking, Cards, & Crafts, Fri Cat Lovers Group Official. Each only after its rules are recorded and allow it.

### When people ask "where did you get it?"

- Answer warmly and plainly, and only in the way the group allows (in the comments, by DM, or "check my profile" if links are banned). Example: "Thank you! I make these at my small business, Memobrick. You upload a photo on our website and see it as a mosaic before you order. Here's the link if you'd like to try yours: <tracked link>".
- If the group bans links even in comments, say "Memobrick, mymemobrick.com" only if that is allowed, otherwise "I'll send it to you" by DM **only when the person asked**. Never DM people who did not ask.
- Product facts only from `marketing/shared/product-facts.md` and that day's Shopify data (for example: the 16×16 kit has 2,500 pieces and takes about 2.5 hours to build, per Shopify's estimate). Unknowns stay unknown (how many faces fit the smaller sizes is not settled).
- Offers only if `marketing/shared/offers-calendar.md` lists them as active and the group allows offers; say a code can't be combined with other offers or free shipping.

### Tracked links

Every link we share in a group carries:

`https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=<group-slug>`

Slugs for the test: `dog-mom-club`, `golden-doodle-owners`, `cat-lovers-group-official`, `craft-creative-ideas-diy`, `scrapbooking-cards-crafts`. Use the editor page (people start their design there) or the gift collection `https://mymemobrick.com/collections/the-gift-for-everyone` for gift questions.

### Measuring it in Shopify

- Shopify Analytics (read-only): sessions, add-to-cart and orders where UTM medium = `group`, split by UTM campaign (one row per group). The planner reads it in its weekly run and writes the numbers into the test log.
- Eden keeps a 1-line daily log per group in `marketing/social/planner/research/facebook-groups-log.md`: posts and comments made, reactions, questions asked, DMs received, anything removed or any warning.
- Many people will search "Memobrick" instead of clicking, so also watch Facebook-referred and direct orders during the test against the two weeks before. This is a hint, not proof.
- **Success after 2 weeks:** at least 1 tracked order, or at least 30 tracked visits and 5 real questions from one group, with no post removed and no warning. Keep the groups that meet it, drop the rest.

### Risks

- **Removal or ban from a group** for breaking a rule we did not see. Mitigation: rules recorded first, one post per group, real project posts only.
- **Spam reports** can lower how Facebook shows the Memobrick Page and Eden's posts, and repeated reports can block posting or link sharing for a while. Mitigation: no repeated links, no copy-paste, never more than one group post a day.
- **Looking like a disguised ad.** Mitigation: Eden always says she runs Memobrick.
- **Time.** About 20 minutes a day for Eden in the test. If it brings no orders in 2 weeks, we stop.
- **Pet loss and grief groups:** posting a product there would hurt people. Out completely.

### Who does the work

- **Buffer cannot post to Facebook groups** (no group posting in its API; it can only remind). So group posts are done **by hand by Eden**, or later by the Mac's Claude in Chrome job, logged in as Eden, **only after a separate approval** for that job. The Mac must never join groups or post on its own.
- The planner prepares each group's post in a kit file (text, photo from the cleared library, tracked link, the group's rule summary) after the test is approved and the rules are recorded.
