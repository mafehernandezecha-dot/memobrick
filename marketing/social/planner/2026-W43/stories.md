# Stories, 2026-W43 (Oct 19 to 25): "Send us your photo" launch

Story ideas for Instagram and Facebook Stories, posted by hand. Not in the calendar. All three ask followers to DM the photo they would love to see in bricks. Never send DMs to followers yourself or in bulk; only reply to people who write to us.

When someone sends a photo: save it to the "submitted photos" Drive folder (once you create it and write its ID under `## Submitted photos` in `marketing/social/footage-rights.md`), and before it is used anywhere send them the permission message in this week's run report. Only photos with written permission recorded as `ok` are used. A photo with a child also needs a parent's or guardian's yes.
