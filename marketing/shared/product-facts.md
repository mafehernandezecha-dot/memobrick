# Product facts

The reference every agent checks before writing about the product or making an image of it. Taken from live Shopify data on 2026-10-12. Prices change: before quoting a price, re-check it in Shopify (read-only) that day, and update this file if it differs.

## The kit (every size)

- A personalized DIY brick mosaic kit made from the customer's photo.
- The customer uploads a photo to the web editor on mymemobrick.com, adjusts colors and details, and previews sizes before ordering.
- In the kit: 1×1 bricks, white baseplates, personalized building instructions (a printed color map), and the building tool. Bricks come in small labeled bags sorted by color (seen in owner footage: a color name and number on each bag).
- Packaging: a white box with a colorful pixel-mosaic graphic and the MEMOBRICK logo (seen in owner footage).
- Frames are sold separately (see Frames). A "Yes" option on most sizes costs more than the build-at-home kit; its exact meaning (most likely delivered already built) is unconfirmed, so do not describe it until the owner confirms.

## What the product looks like (for images and video)

- A flat, square or rectangular mosaic of small 1×1 bricks with round studs on top, every brick the same size, laid in a tight grid. No larger bricks, no plates sticking out, no minifigures.
- The image reads as a pixelated portrait: up close you see each brick, from a distance you see the photo.
- Larger sizes are several baseplates joined edge to edge.
- Product photos to pass as `--ref` to the image tool: Shopify product images, downloaded through the store domain (`https://mymemobrick.com/cdn/shop/files/<file name>?width=1200`, where `<file name>` is the last part of the image URL Shopify returns). The `cdn.shopify.com` address is blocked for agents; the store domain works.

## Not settled (do not claim)

- What the "Yes" option is.
- How many faces fit the 16×16 to 30×20 sizes.
- Shopify's product description says "Let our designers create it for you and receive a free proof". The brand rules say the website creates the design and never to imply a manual proof, so do not repeat that line until the owner settles it.
