# Why 2026 is behind 2025, and what beats last year's holiday

Date: 2026-10-11. Source: Shopify analytics and Meta (PhotoBrick ad account). Net sales exclude tax.

## The decline is long, not one week

| Month | Orders 2025 | Orders 2026 | Net sales 2025 | Net sales 2026 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jun | 236 | 107 | $21,055 | $10,016 | -52% |
| Jul | 192 | 79 | $19,625 | $8,304 | -58% |
| Aug | 228 | 81 | $26,013 | $8,429 | -68% |
| Sep | 177 | 43 | $20,146 | $3,675 | -82% |

Average order value is unchanged (about $95 to $105). The loss is in the number of orders.

## Where the orders went

**1. Fewer mobile shoppers (the biggest loss).** July and August mobile sessions fell from 14,524 (2025) to 7,896 (2026), down 46%. Mobile shoppers who do arrive buy as well as last year: 2.0% complete checkout in 2026 vs 1.8% in 2025. Mobile is where social and ad traffic lands.

**2. Desktop conversion collapsed.** Desktop sessions rose 16% (4,435 to 5,158) but desktop checkouts fell from 56 to 22, and desktop add to cart fell from 7.1% to 2.9%. Either the desktop upload and editor experience is broken, or much of the desktop traffic is junk (bot-like spikes: 868 sessions on Sep 23, 3,000+ sessions in the weeks of Mar 9 and Mar 30 with very few cart additions). This needs a hands-on test.

**3. Google and Facebook orders roughly halved.**

| Source (orders) | Jul to Sep 2025 | Jul to Sep 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Google search | 172 | 80 |
| Facebook | 42 | 19 |

**4. Meta spend fell less than sales.** Jul to Sep spend: $9,987 (2025) vs $6,688 (2026), down 33%, while sales fell about 70%. Blended sales per ad dollar fell from about 7.7 (Aug 2025) to 2.0 (Sep 2026). Spend cuts explain part of the gap, not all of it.

After the cart, things work: the share of carts that turn into orders is similar or better than last year. The problem is getting real shoppers to the site and getting desktop visitors to add to cart.

November and December 2025: 1,099 orders, $114,072 net sales. Meta spend: $4,440 (Nov) and $9,331 (Dec). Demand doubles in the week of Nov 10 and peaks Dec 1 to 14. The week of Dec 15 still brought 167 orders, so an early order-by date gives up real sales.

Best sellers Nov to Dec 2025: 16x16 (303 orders), 20x20 (141), 10x10 one face (118), 20x30 (65).

## Priorities to beat it

1. **Fix desktop before spending more.** Test upload, preview, size choice, and add to cart on desktop browsers this week. Separate junk traffic from real visitors.
2. **Bring back mobile shoppers.** Test Meta creative angles in October (couples, pets, family, Christmas) so winners are ready to scale from Nov 10. Last year's December spend was about twice November's.
3. **Win back Google.** Remove the hardcoded 4.8 / 747 rating from product structured data, publish the Christmas hub page now, and fix duplicate articles. Connect Search Console to see whether Google penalized the site.
4. **Capture the demand that arrives.** Fix abandoned cart links so they restore the cart. Deadline emails performed best last year ("Final Hours": 14 orders).
5. **Set the latest order-by date production allows**, and offer gift cards after it.
