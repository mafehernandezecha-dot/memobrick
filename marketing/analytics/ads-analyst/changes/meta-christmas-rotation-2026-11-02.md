## Why fewer ads

The campaign now has $20 a day. The come-back ad set has a $10/day minimum spend, so the main ad set gets about $10 a day. Seven live ads on $10 a day is about $1.40 per ad per day: no ad gets enough spend to show anything, and Meta keeps relearning. Four ads in the main ad set gives each about $2.50 a day, and the leaders get more. Recommendation: **4 live ads in the main ad set, 2 in the come-back ad set** (plus the no-code come-back ad that starts on its own on Nov 23).

## Expected effect on orders

Honest view: small. At $20 a day this campaign cannot bring many orders whatever the ads. The point is to stop splitting $10 a day across 7 ads and to give the best-ever unused video a fair test. Expected: about the same spend, clearer winners by Nov 9.

## Risk

Low. Swapping ads restarts some learning, and the budget changed on Nov 1 (a big change), so the 7-day quiet period is not over. At $20 a day the campaign cannot finish learning anyway (it needs about 50 purchases a week), so waiting gains nothing.
