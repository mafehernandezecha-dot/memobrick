## Why

From the task board's numbers (Google, Oct 12 to Nov 8): the brand campaign lost 55.5% of its possible impressions to budget, its clicks turn into Google conversions at 9.2%, and a click costs $3.44. Brand searchers already know us, and on Shopify this campaign brought 8 tagged orders in 28 days (Oct 16 to Nov 12, $1,068.53, ROAS 2.35 on last touch, 2.69 counting first touch), and 2 in the last 7 days ($486.31, ROAS 4.32).

## Expected effect on orders (from those three numbers only)

$15 a day / $3.44 per click = about 4.4 extra clicks a day. 4.4 x 9.2% = about 0.4 extra orders a day, about 3 a week, if the budget is the limit.

If the budget is not the limit now, Google will spend little of the extra $15, so it costs little and adds little. The 55.5% figure is mostly from late October, when the budget was lower.

Low. Unused budget is not spent. Google can spend up to twice the daily budget on a busy day, so a single day could reach $120 on this campaign; over a month it stays within 30.4 x $60.
