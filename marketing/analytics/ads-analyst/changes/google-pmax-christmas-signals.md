## Why one asset group and not one per occasion

PMax had 12.5 conversions in 28 days. Splitting that across several occasion groups would starve each of data. Christmas is 44% of the year's orders and the only occasion in the next 8 weeks, so one Christmas group now; Valentine's gets its own group on Jan 15 only if Eden confirms a Valentine's offer, and Mother's Day in April.

## Expected effect on orders

Audience signals steer PMax toward people like our buyers sooner, and a gift-specific group gives Google relevant text and a page that converted 6.3% last holiday. Goal: PMax search impression share from 14% to about 20% by mid-November at the same spend, worth about +1 to 2 orders a week, more in the Nov 17 to Dec 15 peak. Estimate, not a promise.

## Risk

A new asset group can take some impressions from the existing groups for 1 to 2 weeks while it learns. The campaign's total spend cannot rise above its $48 budget (it currently spends about $13 a day).
