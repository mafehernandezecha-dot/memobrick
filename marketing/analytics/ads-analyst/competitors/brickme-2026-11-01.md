## What they do
- All 10 are videos, people on camera (creator/customer style): unboxing, reactions, families building, a finished piece on the wall, a testimonial.
- The first second is a face, a reaction caption ("Wow!", "That's so cool!", "We love our Brick Me!") or a push-in from the finished mosaic to the bricks (#2).
- One simple primary text reused word for word on 6 of 10: what it is, upload/design/order, kit like paint-by-numbers, "Upload + design for free!". They test videos, not copy.
- No price, discount, code or deadline in any of the 10. Offer = free design / free preview.
- Most scaled: #3 "The perfect gift doesn't exi-" son's birthday unboxing (4 ads), #7 gift angle for grandparents and pet parents (3 ads), #4 family unboxing (2 ads).
- Longest running: #1 since Sep 12, LEGO Masters contestants unboxing (celebrity proof).
