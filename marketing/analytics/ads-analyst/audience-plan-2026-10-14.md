## The short version

You are right: traffic is down a lot. Visits from Google search fell 76%, from Facebook 52%, from Instagram 59% (Sep 14 to Oct 13, this year vs last). The two big reasons are simple:

1. **Google shows our ads far less often, and the cause is ad quality, not money.** PMax appears for 14% of the searches it could; 86% of the misses are lost to ad rank. Holiday Gifts: 12% shown, 76% lost to rank. Budgets are not the limit (PMax spends about $13 of its $48 a day).
2. **Meta spends less than half of last year, on weaker ads.** $2,151 vs $4,835 in the same 30 days, and none of today's ads mention Christmas, our order-by date, the live 20% code or FOX. Last holiday's best ad (the couple video) made 128 Meta purchases at $29 each; it is running again but at a tiny budget.

The plan below changes who we talk to and what we say, inside today's budget.

## 1. Traffic diagnosis (plain English)

### Where the visits went (Shopify sessions, Sep 14 to Oct 13)

| Source | 2025 | 2026 | Change | Add-to-cart rate 2025 / 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google search (paid and organic mixed) | 2,695 | 655 | -76% | 7.2% / 7.8% |
| Facebook (paid and organic mixed) | 1,837 | 877 | -52% | 8.9% / 3.8% |
| Instagram (paid and organic mixed) | 651 | 265 | -59% | 3.7% / 3.8% |

Google visitors are as good as ever (they add to cart even a bit more often); we just get a quarter of them. Facebook visitors are fewer and half as ready to buy, which points at the ads and landing pages, not the audience size.

### Google: impression share lost to rank vs budget

| Campaign | Shown | Lost to budget | Lost to rank | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PMax #2 | 14% | 0.1% | about 86% | Money is not the problem. Google thinks our ads and pages are less relevant than competitors'. |
| Holiday Gifts | 12% | 11% | about 76% | Same. Generic copy and a product page instead of the gift collection. |
| Brand | 84% | n/a | up to 16% | Fine. |
| NonBrand (paused Oct 12) | 11% | 84% | 6% | The only budget-starved campaign, but it bought LEGO and freebie clicks with 0 sales in 28 days. Pausing it cost some visits, not sales. |

- **CTR:** Brand 37.8%, PMax 1.6%, Holiday Gifts 3.9%, NonBrand 12.3% (Oct 12 pull). Holiday Gifts' low click rate is part of its low rank: occasion copy (action 3) is the free fix.
- **How to win back rank without more spend:** (a) send gift searches to the gift collection, which converted 6.3% last holiday vs 2.3% for today's page (landing page experience is part of rank); (b) write ads that repeat the searcher's words (gift for mom, grandparents, pet, Christmas photo gift); (c) block searches that never buy (LEGO, free, printable) so the click rate and conversion rate the bidding sees improve; (d) give PMax a buyer signal and a gift-specific asset group; (e) Merchant Center product titles that lead with what people search ("Custom Photo Brick Mosaic Kit 20x20 in, Personalized Photo Gift") and never "LEGO". None of these raises spend.
- **Google spend:** about $100 a day in early August, $10 to $25 in mid-September, about $30 to $35 a day now. Last year's Google spend and impression share for the same weeks are not read yet (requested for tomorrow's pull).

### Meta: delivery and frequency

| | Sep 14 to Oct 13, 2025 | 2026 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spend | $4,835 | $2,151 | -56% |
| People reached | 120,416 | 70,197 | -42% |
| Link clicks | 2,715 | 1,012 | -63% |
| Landing page views | 2,534 (93% of clicks) | 773 (76% of clicks) | -69% |
| Cost per click | $1.78 | $2.13 | +20% |
| Frequency | 2.20 | 1.68 | no ad fatigue |

Plain English: about four fifths of the Meta traffic drop is simply less money; the rest is weaker ads (lower click rate) and more clicks that never load the page (76% of clicks reach the page vs 93% last year, worth a mobile speed check by site-developer). Frequency is low, so people are not tired of us; we just reach fewer of them. Meta's own trend check says the main ad (FAM BLUE BACKGORUND) lost 31% of its click rate and doubled its cost per click over the last period.

Last holiday Meta spent about $134 a day in one campaign (oct_29) at Meta ROAS 3.49. Today Meta spends about $60 a day in total. **Without more budget, Meta traffic cannot reach last year's volume.** What we can do inside $60 is put the money on the ads and people that bought (actions 1 and 2) and prove a Shopify ROAS near 3.0 by mid-November, so you can decide the holiday budget on evidence (the "Holiday ad budget" fact is still unknown).
