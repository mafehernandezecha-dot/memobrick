## First screen compared

- Dog page (phone): "Your dog, in 1x1 bricks", photo of a real kit next to a dog, one red button "Upload your dog's photo" (y=587), "Opens on the 20x20 kit ($99.88). You can switch sizes in the editor.", 4.9 from 1,027 reviews, then "As seen on FOX" and "Free design proof". Strong, and it has what the editor lacks (price, reviews, the dog).
- Editor (phone, `inapp-fb-android-editor-first.jpg`): "Turn your favourite photo into brick art.", three steps, "We never share your photo", an upload box. No price, no reviews, no dog. The same screen is shown after tapping the dog button (`inapp-fb-android-dog-editor.jpg`): the dog message and the price are dropped at the moment the visitor must choose a photo.
- Conclusion: the dog page is the better first screen; the one weak spot is the hand-off to the editor, which is what proposal CA-20261031-01 already fixes. I am not proposing a second change: the page itself passes and a second proposal on the same page would muddy the test.
