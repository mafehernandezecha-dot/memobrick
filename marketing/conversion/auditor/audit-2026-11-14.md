## 2. Check against buyers: paid web orders Nov 1 to 13 under $125

Same fields, Online Store orders only (draft orders and $0 orders left out).

| Group | Orders | Shipping 15%+ of subtotal | Median shipping | Median shipping % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Abandoned, kit carts under $125 (above) | 12 | 9 (75%) | $12.85 | 20.0% |
| Paid, carts under $125 | 16 | 10 (63%) | $11.18 | 16.4% |
| Paid, carts $125+ that used a code (so paid shipping) | 7 | 0 | $15.75 | 13.2% |

What this says:
- Shipping takes a large share of most small carts, and buyers paid it too: 10 of 16 paid
  under-$125 orders also paid 15% or more. Shipping cost alone does not separate the people
  who left from the people who paid on the 16x16 and 20x20.
- **The 10x10 mini is the exception.** At $35 its shipping was $12.08 to $15.53 (34% to 44%).
  It has 3 abandoned checkouts with shipping (all Nov 11 and 12) plus 1 more without an address,
  and **0 orders of the 10x10 on any channel in Nov 1 to 13** (Shopify sales by product; the
  20x20 had 10 in the same query). For a $35 gift, shipping adds more than a third, and nobody finished.
- Medians differ by about $1.70 and 4 points. With 12 and 16 carts that is a hint, not proof.

## 3. What this means for the 43% to 33% checkout drop (for Eden's decision)

- Shipping is part of the drop, mostly on the cheapest kit. On the 16x16 and 20x20 the same
  shipping share is paid by most buyers, so it is unlikely to be the main reason there.
- 4 of the 12 abandoned kit carts used a code; on the $125+ carts, the code removing free
  shipping remains the clearest shipping surprise (pending A-2026W46-017, the cart line).
- Options that are the owner's call (shipping rates are an owner decision, Facts 2026-10-12;
  no proposal today): a flat US rate for the 10x10 mini only, or a lower free-shipping threshold
  test for Black Friday weeks. Either one I can measure on the 10x10 checkouts from the day it
  goes live.
