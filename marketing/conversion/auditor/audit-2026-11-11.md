## 1. The May 18 case: the lost draft orders were not free-proof orders

Read-only GraphQL on draft orders (only date, amount, line item name, payment gateway, invoice date; no customer fields).

There are two kinds of draft orders:

| Kind | What it looks like | Paid how | Invoice sent |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Manually keyed orders | Custom line item typed by staff ("memobrick 16x16", "memobrick 20x20", "memobrick 30x40"), common totals $82.10, $112.20, $162.60 | `manual` gateway, marked paid 4 to 20 seconds after creation | never |
| B. Free-proof / invoice orders | Real product variant ("Custom Photo Brick Mosaic Portrait 20x20 in ... No (Build at home)") | `shopify_payments`, customer pays the invoice minutes to days later | yes, from Aug 30 2026 on |
| C. Replacement parts | "missing 2 caramel", "missing 6 bags of red" | $0 | never |

Counts:

| Window | A. manual paid | B. Shopify Payments | C. $0 parts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oct 12 to Nov 10 2025 | 51 (total $6,595.44, about 1.7 a day) | 5 | 3 |
| Apr 13 to 24 2026 | 28 custom "memobrick NxN" lines (gateway checked on 12 of them: all manual) | 0 | 2 |
| May 4 to May 17 2026 | 16 | 3 | 4 |
| May 18 to May 25 2026 | 6 (last ones: May 25, $96.80 and $154.50) | 1 | 0 |
| May 26 to Sep 30 2026 | **0** | 15 | 15 |
| Oct 1 to Nov 10 2026 | **0** | 11 (10 paid, 1 open) | 5 |

What this means:
- The collapse is kind A only: staff stopped keying "memobrick NxN" orders paid outside the Shopify checkout. The last one was May 25 2026. Before that, about 8 to 14 a week were entered in batches on weekday afternoons, every week from at least Oct 2025.
- In the same 30 days last year these were 51 orders and $6,595, which is **44% of the 116-order gap** (188 orders Oct 12 to Nov 10 2025 vs 72 Oct 11 to Nov 9 2026, `sales/latest.json`).
- The free-proof path is healthy, not broken: invoice orders (kind B) are up, 9 paid in Oct 11 to Nov 10 2026 vs 5 in the same days of 2025, and Hulk form submissions in May and June 2026 were 32 and 30 (Hulk monthly emails, audit 2026-10-26), higher than 2025. If the form had broken on May 18, those would have dropped too.
- So the question for Eden is not "what broke on the site" but **"which sales channel did the manual orders come from, and why did it stop after May 25?"** Their prices ($82.10 for a 16x16, $112.20 for a 20x20) do not match the site's prices plus Florida tax (that gives $74.77 and $106.87), so they were priced somewhere else: a marketplace, a reseller or wholesale partner, phone or chat orders, or orders entered for someone else. Only Eden knows. If that channel can be switched back on before Black Friday, it is worth more than any site fix in this audit.
