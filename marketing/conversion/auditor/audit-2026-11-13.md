## Top 3 things that could stop add to cart (ranked)

1. **No Christmas date on the page where gift traffic lands.** Black Friday shoppers buying for Christmas have to open a product and scroll about 3 screens to learn they can still get it in time ("Order by December 15, 2026", Facts table). The collection page says nothing about timing. Evidence: no date text on the collection; the date is at y 2,717 on the 20x20 product page. Exposure: every one of about 285 phone sessions a week, rising when A-2026W46-007 moves Meta traffic here.
2. **Shipping cost is unknown until the cart.** The collection and product pages never mention free US shipping at $125 or more. The best seller (20x20, $99.88) and the 16x16 ($69.88) are under it, so most carts pay live carrier rates that first appear in the cart or at checkout. This weighs more on checkout than on add to cart: 17 phone checkouts became 4 orders this week, against 15 to 8 last year (abandoned checkouts with shipping added, Nov 12 audit).
3. **Nothing to tap on the first screen, and sizes are hard to compare.** The first screen is a title, the stars and one photo. The header "Create My MemoBrick" button is hidden on phone, and the free proof link is 13 screens down. Picking a size means reading long titles on 1.5 tiles per screen; only the 10x10 says how many faces fit. Evidence: `phone-gift-01-first-screen.jpg`, the scroll screenshots, and the tile titles in `phone-gift.json`.

## Proposal

**No new proposal.** Gaps 1 and 3 are exactly what the pending **A-2026W45-009** (`site-changes/collection-gift-start-block.md`) fixes, in one theme-copy change to this collection only. It adds the instruction line, "Order by December 15 to get it before Christmas", a red "Create My MemoBrick" button and the free proof link on the first screen. A second proposal would duplicate it. It expires Nov 20 and needs a free theme slot (20 of 20 used).

The test numbers have changed since it was proposed. At 285 phone sessions a week, the 120 sessions per arm in CA-20261103-01 take about 6 days, not 3 weeks, so it can be decided before Nov 27 if it is live by Nov 20. Arm A already converts 10.5%, so the "at least 7%" floor no longer binds, and the 1.3x rule decides.

Gap 2 is partly covered by pending A-2026W46-017 (P-conversion-auditor-20261112-1) (the cart line "free US shipping" and that codes don't combine). The collection page itself still says nothing. I did not add a proposal for it, to stay within one proposal per task.
