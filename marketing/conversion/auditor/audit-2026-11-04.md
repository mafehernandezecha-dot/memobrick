By source: direct 289, social 10 (0 carts), search 1. This is why "referred" counts show only about 2 a week: nobody sends people to this page. No sessions landed here last year (the page is new), so there is no year-over-year. Oct 21 to Nov 3 daily sessions: 2 to 17, last three days 16, 17, 14 (up), carts Oct 26 to 31: 1 in about 47 sessions, Nov 1 to 3: 4 in 47.

Why people do not arrive: no link, ad, post or search result points to it (7 non-direct sessions in 30 days). Why they do not start: they mostly do, phone 11.2% add to cart is the best entry we have. The weak side is desktop (3.5%) and the first screen for a cold visitor.

First screen, phone (`phone-editor-bare-first.jpg`) and desktop (`desktop-editor-bare-first.jpg`): "Back" link, three-step bar, headline "Turn your favourite photo into brick art.", one sentence, three chips, upload box. No price, no date, no proof offer, no reviews. Below the box: free-photo search, a Cmd V / Ctrl V keyboard tip that is useless on a phone, three photo-tip cards, and "Not sure your photo will work? let us design it for you" as the last line, about 2,300 px down (`phone-editor-bare-full.jpg`, page 3,149 px tall). Loads 3.9 to 5.1 s on phone in this sandbox (first load, includes the editor script), 4 s with `?size=20x20#editor`. No horizontal scroll at 390 px. The "Back" link goes to the home page, so a visitor who is unsure leaves the purchase path.

Note: the live page says "We never share your photo", the repo theme says "Your photo stays private", so the live theme differs from this repo copy.
