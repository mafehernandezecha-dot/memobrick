correction, and what the cart tells visitors. Full weekly audit stays on Wednesday (Nov 18).

## 1. Correction to the Nov 11 audit

The Nov 11 audit said "no free-shipping rule found in the delivery profile or active automatic
discounts". That was wrong. Free US shipping on orders of $125 or more exists as the automatic
discount "Free Shipping on US Orders Over $125" (active since 2026-08-11, offers-calendar Facts),
and discount codes do not combine with it (owner decision, 2026-10-20). Proof it works: an
abandoned $149 20x30 checkout of Oct 28 shows a $12.85 shipping discount and a $149.00 total.
So a buyer using EARLYHOLIDAY20 or any other code pays live carrier shipping, even over $125.

report). It inflates the abandoned checkout count and is not a site problem.
