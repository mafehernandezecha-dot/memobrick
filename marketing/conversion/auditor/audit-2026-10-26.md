## Key finding: the draft-order drop is the Etsy shop, not the free-proof form

Last year most "Draft Orders" were Etsy sales that staff keyed into Shopify for fulfillment, marked paid with the `manual` payment method. The Etsy shop was permanently suspended on 2026-05-18 for intellectual property reports (Etsy Trust & Safety email that day; appeals denied May 22 and Jul 30). The last manual-paid order in Shopify is 2026-05-25. Since June there are none.
