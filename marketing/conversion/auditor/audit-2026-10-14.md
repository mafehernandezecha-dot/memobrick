### Where the drop is largest

1. Traffic: referred sessions are down 65% (1,922 against 5,452). This is the biggest loss and is not a site problem.
2. Add to cart: 5.1% against 7.3% for referred sessions. Worst on Facebook (3.8% against 8.9% last year) and Instagram (3.8%, no purchases). Google is on par (7.8%).
3. After add to cart the site works: 7-day phone checkout to purchase is 16 of 26, better than last year (33 of 76).
4. Draft orders (designer proof then invoice) are still near zero (see weak points). The owner steps from the 2026-10-12 report are unchanged and still open.
