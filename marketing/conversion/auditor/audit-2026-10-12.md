# Conversion audit 2026-10-12 (first run)

Agent: conversion-auditor (Wilson). Live store https://mymemobrick.com tested with Playwright (Chromium) at phone size (390×844) and desktop size (1440×900). Funnel data comes from Shopify analytics (read-only, ShopifyQL). No payment details were entered and no order was placed: every walk stopped at the checkout page.

## The short version

1. **"Visitors are up" is not real traffic.** About 6,300 of the 10,630 sessions in the last 30 days are desktop "direct" sessions with almost no carts. 3,033 US desktop direct sessions landed on the home page and added 5 carts. 994 landed on one 10×10 "yin-yang" product page and added 0. **Real (referred) visitors fell 63%**: 1,910 against 5,158 last year. Google fell 76% and Facebook 45%.
2. **The designer-proof sales channel has nearly stopped.** Draft-order sales, where the team sends a proof and then an invoice, fell to 3 orders against 67 in the same 30 days last year. By sales channel the store took 51 orders against 170 last year: draft orders account for 64 of the 119 missing orders and the online store for 53. Monthly draft orders went from 34 in May 2026 to 9 in June and have stayed under 10 since.
3. **On the site itself, the drop is at add to cart, mostly on phones.** Referred phone sessions add to cart at 5.5% against 6.9% last year, and all phone sessions at 4.8% against 7.9% in the last 7 days. Once something is in the cart, people buy as well as last year or better: 26% of checkouts complete against 21%.
4. The walk found friction that explains part of the phone and desktop add-to-cart drop. On the home page, the phone button is below the first screen. Two pop-ups come in a row after upload. On desktop, the editor hides its own price and Finish button. Five site changes are proposed below.

### Where the drop is largest

- **Lost orders in total:** 64 of the 119 missing orders (by sales channel) are draft orders, a sales channel rather than a theme step. The online store lost 53, mostly from fewer real visitors (down 63%) and a lower phone add-to-cart rate.
- **Site funnel step:** visit to add to cart. Among real phone visitors it fell from 6.9% to 5.5%, and among home-page phone visitors from 12.5% to 6.6%. Cart to checkout (83%) and checkout to purchase (26%) are equal to or better than last year.

What works well: the photo upload and preview are instant (0.3 s) on both devices. The editor tools (crop, colors) work on a phone. Finish Editing adds to cart reliably on every run (5 of 5). The cart drawer shows the free-shipping progress. Trust signals are present on product pages: the 4.8 rating, free proof, Made & shipped from Miami, and secure checkout.

## 4. Differences from last year that the data shows

- Last year the second-biggest landing page was the 16×16 product inside the gift collection: 1,353 sessions, 5.8% add to cart, 9 purchases. This year it got 177 sessions at 2.8%. Product pages now send people to a separate editor page; the old flow cannot be seen.
- Last year draft-order invoices (designer proof first, pay after approval) brought 67 orders a month. Pro Design now takes payment before the proof.
- The "free shipping over $125" discount started on 2026-08-11.
