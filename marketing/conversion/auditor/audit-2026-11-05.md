## /pages/editor first screen, desktop vs phone

Oct 6 to Nov 4: phone 216 sessions, 23 carts (10.6%), 16 checkout, 5 purchases. Desktop 81 sessions, 3 carts (3.7%), 3 checkout, 1 purchase. Desktop daily sessions are 1 to 6, so the page is nearly unvisited by desktop users.

Screenshots: `2026-11-05/desktop-editor-first.jpg`, `phone-editor-first.jpg`, full pages `*-editor-full.jpg`. Load: phone 2.9 s, desktop 2.5 s (under 3 s).

Above the fold, same words on both: headline, one sentence, three chips (never share photo, instant preview, 40+ colors), upload box. Neither shows a price, a date, reviews or the proof offer (that is CA-20261104-01, already proposed). Differences:

1. Desktop header: seven links and a red "Create My MemoBrick" pill. The pill links to `/pages/editor`, the page you are on, and is the loudest button on screen. Phone header: logo, menu icon, cart.
2. Desktop headline wraps with a one-word second line ("art."), pushing the upload box to y=581 and cutting the "No photo to hand? Search" row at the fold (y=885 of 900). On the phone the box starts at y=538 and the search row is visible at 811.
3. "Back" goes to the home page on both.
4. The help link ("let us design it for you") is at y=1530 on desktop and y=2661 on phone, below the fold on both.
5. The date, Dec 15, is absent here but present on the home page ("Order by December 15 to have it before Christmas") on both devices.

Phone sample (216) is solid; desktop (81) is small but 3 carts against an expected 9 is unlikely to be chance (about 2 in 100). Cause is a hypothesis (item 1 and 2); it is a layout reading, not a measured test.
