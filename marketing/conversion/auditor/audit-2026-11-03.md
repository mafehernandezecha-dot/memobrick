For reference the home page is 6.1% (45 of 736) and search-landing sessions are 10.6%.

First screen on phone (`phone-gift-first.jpg`, 390x844): small "MEMOBRICK" label, title "Custom Photo Brick Mosaic Kits", "4.8 · 747 MemoBrick reviews", then one tile (10x10, $35.00). No button, no instruction, no date. The header shows only a menu and a cart; the "Create My MemoBrick" button appears only in the desktop header (`desktop-gift-first.jpg`). Load 3.9 s on the first phone load, 2.0 s on desktop (a second run of the product page was 2.7 to 3.3 s).

Product order: 10x10 One Face ($35), 16x16 ($69.88), 20x20 ($99.88), 20x30 ($149), then 21 more sizes up to 70x60 ($978) and a gift card in the middle of the list. 25 tiles, about 10,900 px on a phone, titles of 8 to 12 words each. Prices are visible on each tile; no "popular" marker. "December 15" does not appear on the page. The proof offer appears only in the footer link "Get My Free Proof".

Tile behaviour: every tile opens the product page (`/products/2-2-boards` etc.), not the editor. The theme file routes tiles to the editor only when the title contains "Custom Bricked Mosaic" or "Custom Brick Portrait", and the live titles say "Custom Photo Brick Mosaic", so that shortcut no longer matches. The product page itself is strong (reviews, "Real customer photos", "Free proof before production", "Made & shipped from Miami" on the Facts-table claim, price $99.88 with the $159 compare price, sticky red "Design this in the creator" button, December 15 in the page text). Product pages convert at 12 to 20% where traffic exists (`1-big-board` 5 of 25), so the weak step is the collection first screen and its length, not the product page.

Conclusion: the one change most likely to raise this page's cart rate is a start block above the grid with the instruction, the true Dec 15 date, a red "Create My MemoBrick" button and the free proof link. Proposed as `site-changes/collection-gift-start-block.md` (A/B test CA-20261103-01).
