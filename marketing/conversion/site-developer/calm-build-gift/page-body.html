How to use the plan in 3 steps

  1. Before you start. Print the plan and cut out the cards. Pick a quiet table with good light and a session length. 15 or 20 minutes is a good start.
  2. Each session. Open one color bag. Follow the map one row at a time and tick each finished row on the tracker.
  3. When it's time to stop. Put the STOP HERE card on the table, leave the next bag ready and write down the rows done today. Stopping is part of the plan.

Building together

Try three jobs:

The map readerReads the next row from the color map.
The color finderFinds the right color bag and hands over the bricks.
The builderPresses the bricks onto the baseplate.

Swap jobs every few rows, or keep the job you like best. Building alone works just as well: one person does all three, one row at a time.

Questions

Is this a therapy or a treatment?

No. It is a building kit and a free printable plan. We don't claim it treats or improves anything. It is simply a calm, predictable project with a clear finish.

How long does it take to build?

About 2.5 hours for the 16×16, or 50 rows. The plan splits it into short sessions, so you can do a few rows a day with no rush.

Are all the bricks the same?

Yes. Every brick is a 1×1 brick, so the motion is the same every time. Only the color changes, and each bag is labeled with a color name and number that match the map.

What photo works best?

A clear, close-up photo with one main subject works well on the 16×16. You see the mosaic in the editor before you order, so you can try a few photos and pick the one you like.

Do I need the plan to build the kit?

No. The kit comes with its own printed color map. The plan is a free extra for anyone who likes short sessions, a tracker and clear stop points.
