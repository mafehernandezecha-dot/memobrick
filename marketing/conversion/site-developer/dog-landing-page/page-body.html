Questions

Which dog photos work best?

A sharp, bright photo where your dog's face fills most of the picture and both eyes are clear. A photo taken at your dog's eye level works better than one from above, and a simple background helps the face stand out. Skip dark, blurry or far-away shots. You see the mosaic before you order, so try a few.

Which size should I pick?

For one dog's face, the 16×16 and the 20×20 both work well. The 20×20 has 4,096 bricks instead of 2,500, so it shows more detail. The 30×30 is a big wall piece. You can compare sizes in the editor before you order.

How long does it take to build?

About 2.5 hours for the 16×16, 4 hours for the 20×20 and 9 hours for the 30×30. You can build a few rows at a time.

When will it arrive?

Order by December 15 to receive it before Christmas. Kits are made and shipped from Miami, Florida.

How much is shipping?

US orders of $125 and up ship free. Below that, the cost depends on your address, and you see it before you pay. Discount codes can't be combined with free shipping.
