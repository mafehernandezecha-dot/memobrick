## What the chat can do today (checked 2026-11-03)

Sources: Shopify Help Center pages on Inbox instant answers, chat settings and the Inbox agent, read through web search (help.shopify.com itself is blocked from this session, so field lengths and button labels are confirmed in admin by the Chrome prompt, not guessed).

| Feature | What it does | Where | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greeting message | The first message a visitor sees when they open the chat | Theme editor: Online Store > Themes > live theme > Customize > App embeds > Shopify Inbox | free |
| Instant answers | A question list shown under the greeting. The visitor taps a question and gets the answer at once, with no staff needed. Unlimited answers, up to 100 shown. Persona rules for phase 2 (to paste into the persona when Eden approves it):

## Instant answers (in display order)

### 1. How does it work?
Upload a photo in our Creator and see it as a brick mosaic in seconds. Pick your size and order. We send 1×1 bricks sorted in labeled color bags, white baseplates, your personal color map and a building tool, and you build it at home. Start here: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor

### 2. Can I see my mosaic before I buy?
Yes, for free. Upload your photo in the Creator and the mosaic preview appears right away, with nothing to pay. Try different sizes and crops until it looks right, then order when you're happy: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor

### 3. Will it arrive before Christmas?
Order by December 15 to receive it before Christmas. Each kit is made to order and shipped from Miami, and you get a tracking link by email as soon as it ships. Ordering later? The Art Gift Kit lets you give it now and choose the photo after: https://mymemobrick.com/products/unlimited-memobrick

### 4. What size should I choose?
For one face, 10×10 or 16×16. For two people, 20×20. For a family or group, 20×30 or larger, up to 60×60 for up to 9 people. Bigger sizes show more detail. Upload your photo and the Creator suggests the best size: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor

### 5. How much is it and what's included?
Kits start at $35 (10×10). For example: 16×16 $69.88, 20×20 $99.88, 30×30 $209.99. Every kit has the 1×1 bricks in labeled color bags, white baseplates, your printed color map and a building tool. Frames are sold separately. See every size and price: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor

### 6. Can you design it for me?
Yes. Send us your photo and Memobrick's designers will create the design and email you a free proof before production. It's optional and there's nothing to pay for the proof: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/free-preview

### 7. It's a gift. What if I don't have their photo?
Give the Art Gift Kit: buy now, no photo needed. We email you a personalization link, one per kit, to forward whenever you like. They choose their photo and approve the design, then we make and ship their kit: https://mymemobrick.com/products/unlimited-memobrick

### 8. How much is shipping?
US orders of $125 or more ship free. Under $125, checkout shows the live USPS and UPS rates for your address before you pay. Discount codes can't be combined with free shipping, so you get one or the other.

### 9. Which photo works best?
One clear subject filling most of the photo, with light on the faces and a quiet background. Close crops work best, and you can zoom and crop in the Creator. Tip: look at the preview from arm's length. Try yours: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor

### 10. Is there a holiday offer? (time-limited: turn off on November 23)
Through November 22, use code EARLYHOLIDAY20 for 20% off. It can't be combined with other offers or with free shipping on orders of $125 and up, so you get one or the other. Start your design: https://mymemobrick.com/pages/editor

### 11. How long does it take to build? Is it OK for kids?
About an hour per 1,000 bricks: 16×16 takes around 2.5 hours, 20×20 around 4, 30×30 around 9. Your color map guides you one plate at a time, so it's easy to follow and fun to build together. The 1×1 bricks are small, so little ones should build with an adult.

### 12. Is a frame included?
Frames are sold separately, in black, for most sizes (for example 16×16 $49, 20×20 $65, 30×30 $85). You can add one to the same order: https://mymemobrick.com/collections/photo-frames

### 13. What happens to my photo?
Your preview is built in your browser. We keep a copy of your photo and design so we can help you finish it and make your order. We never share or sell it.

### 14. What if a piece is missing?
Sorry about that! Send us your order number, the color name and number from the bag label, and a photo of your build, here or at support@memobrickofficial.com. Missing, damaged or defective pieces are replaced or refunded, as our refund policy explains: https://mymemobrick.com/policies/refund-policy

### 15. Can I return it?
Each kit is made from your photo, so custom kits can be refunded in full any time before the order ships, but not after. Missing, damaged or defective pieces are replaced or refunded. Frames are final sale. Details: https://mymemobrick.com/policies/refund-policy

## Fix before the Inbox agent (phase 2): store text that disagrees
The Inbox agent reads these, so each one could become a wrong answer:
- Home page FAQ "What if a brick is missing?" says replacements go out free "for as long as you own the kit". The refund policy summary says missing pieces are replaced or refunded within a 14-day window. Eden decides which is true.
- Refund policy: the summary box says custom kits get a full refund before shipping; the policy text below it says most products are refundable with a $20 restocking fee. They need to say the same thing.
- Shipping policy says "We currently ship within the United States only" and also offers international shipping, says orders are processed in 1 to 2 business days, and still has a Covid-19 notice.
- Home page and Creator say kits ship "in 2–4 business days" (on the brand-conflicts list).
- /pages/faq is a 404, so there is no FAQ page for the agent to read; the Knowledge Base app is the clean way to give it these 15 answers.
