Definitions:
- Delivered = sent minus failed.
- Purchases = attributed orders (Omnisend attribution).
- Purchases per delivered email = purchases / delivered.
- Revenue per recipient = flow attributed revenue / delivered count of the flow's first email (closest available proxy for unique people who entered; Omnisend reports has no unique-recipient metric).
- Opens shown only as a secondary signal.

Important caveat: every in-scope flow was disabled and re-enabled on 2026-09-28, and every email's content was updated on 2026-09-24 to 2026-09-28. Most of this 90-day window reflects the old content. Only about two weeks of data describe what is live now. All numbers are small; treat differences of one or two orders as noise.

## Brand and honesty blockers found in live emails

These appear across most in-scope emails and conflict with `memobrick-brand`. They are not ranked by purchases, but any test should start only after the owner decides on them, so both test arms share the same compliant base.

1. "Ships in 2–4 days, From Miami, tracked" and "made and shipped from Miami within 2–4 business days": the Miami claim is unknown in the Facts table and no delivery timing is listed there.
2. "As seen on Fox News, NBC and Yahoo": no source on file.
3. "4.8 from 740+ verified reviews": not in the Facts table. Allowed only if it matches the public Loox count.
4. "Free proof first. Nothing is made until you approve" and "a real Memobrick designer will build your mosaic": the brand rules say never imply a manual proof is required or that a designer personally created the design.
5. Named customer quotes: allowed only if they are public Loox reviews or recorded in `marketing/conversion/reviews-ugc/approved-stories.md` (that file does not exist in the repo).
6. Welcome 20% code: not listed as an active offer in `offers-calendar.md`.
7. "Free replacements, any missing brick, for life": not in the Facts table.

## Top three improvements, ranked by expected purchases

1. **Abandoned Cart: send shoppers back to their own cart.** Replace the plain `/cart` links with a cart-restore link (the cart-event equivalent of `abandoned_checkout_url`; exact merge tag to be confirmed in Omnisend). Why: cart email 1 has the most clicks of any recovery email (10) but 0 purchases. The checkout flow, which restores the session, converts 2.4% per delivered email. If cart emails 1 and 2 matched even half the checkout rate, that is about 2 to 3 more purchases per 90 days. This is a link fix, not a test.
2. **Recovery last email: test reassurance against the 20% discount.** The two discount emails delivered 164 times with 1 click and 0 purchases, and the discount has a margin cost (margins unknown). Proposed test: `test-cart-last-email-reassurance.md`. Also fix the checkout email 3 discount button so it goes to the checkout URL, not /cart.
3. **Welcome email 2: give it one job.** 165 delivered, 1 click, 0 purchases, against 6 purchases from email 1. Make the 20% code and a single "Start designing" CTA the focus, reuse the code from email 1 instead of generating a second one, and use only verified claims. Expected gain is about 1 to 2 purchases per 90 days at current signup volume.

Smaller items: check the checkout email 1 failures (7.8%); add the line-item design-image fallback to the checkout flow; restyle or retire the default Back in Stock template.
