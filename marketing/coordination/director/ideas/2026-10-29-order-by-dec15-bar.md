# Idea: "Order by Dec 15" delivery bar

- Executor: site-developer (builds an unpublished copy in a theme copy, then proposes the publish herself).
- What: a thin announcement bar on the home page, the dog page, ready-made design pages and the cart: "Order by December 15 to get it before Christmas. Made and shipped from Miami." Uses only Facts-table claims (Dec 15 date, Miami, verified 2026-10-11). No discount code, no countdown timer, no urgency that is not true.
- Why: Gift buyers' main doubt is "will it arrive". Add to cart is 6.1% vs 8.4% last year. Cheap, reversible, applies to all traffic.
- Expected effect: small lift in add to cart on all pages; to be measured as add to cart on referred sessions before and after (growth-analyst).
- Nothing goes live on approval of this idea; Jenny proposes the publish as her own site-change item.
