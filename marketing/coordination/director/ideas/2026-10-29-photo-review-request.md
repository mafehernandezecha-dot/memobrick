# Idea: photo review request to past buyers

- Executor: email-flow-optimizer (designs the test), after Eden confirms the review app can request photos.
- What: one email to buyers from the last 12 months asking for a photo of the finished mosaic on the wall (Loox photo review). No discount for the review, no incentive claims.
- Why: real customer photos are the best proof on the dog page, article cards and ads (Loox shows 4.9 from 1,027 reviews, but few photos on the new pages). It also supplies permission-based photos for the "We brick your pet" series and Meta ads, which replace stock-looking creatives.
- Expected effect: more customer photos for pages and ads within 2 weeks; indirect lift in add to cart and in ad click-to-cart. Not a direct order driver.
- This touches a customer email, so it goes to Eden as a proposal. It must not touch Post-Purchase order emails.
