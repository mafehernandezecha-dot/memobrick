✦ Why does this matter? ✦

MemoBrick turns your photo into tiny bricks. These three things help us capture the most detail and get the best result.

Seven moves that fix almost everything

  1. Crop in first. Use + and drag the picture until the subject fills the board. Do this before anything else — every other setting depends on it.
  2. Can't bear to crop? Hit Fit whole photo and choose a border brick. The margin becomes part of the design instead of a bad crop.
  3. Leave Auto levels on. It rescues flat, hazy and underexposed phone photos before a single brick is chosen. Turn it off only if your photo is already graded.
  4. Push Detail to 40–60% for faces. It sharpens at stud resolution, which is what keeps eyes and jawlines readable. Drop it near 0 for logos and flat graphics.
  5. Keep Blend shades around 70–85% for people. It mixes neighbouring bricks into shades we don't stock. For type, logos or bold graphics, drop it to 0 for clean edges.
  6. Judge it with Step-back view. Mosaics are built up close and looked at from across a room. Nose-to-screen is the wrong distance to make decisions at.
  7. Fix the last few studs by hand. Touch up bricks lets you place individual bricks — an eye that's gone soft or a stray dark stud on a cheek takes about thirty seconds.
